Smashed Cucumber Salad with Chili Oil

Cucumbers smashed with a rolling pin to break the cells open + drink the dressing — soy, vinegar, garlic, sesame and a slick of chili crisp.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 250 g Cucumber
  • 1 tsp Toasted sesame seeds
  • few drops Fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp Chili crisp
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp Soy sauce
  • small handful Fresh coriander
  • pinch Salt
  • ½ tsp Sugar

Instructions

  1. Lay a cucumber on a board, cover with cling film. Smash with a rolling pin or bottom of a saucepan until split + cracked. Tear into rough 2cm chunks (uneven edges grip dressing).
  2. Toss cucumber with 0.5 tsp salt in a sieve. Sit 10 min — the salt pulls out water. Pat dry with paper.
  3. Whisk soy + rice vinegar + sesame oil + grated garlic + sugar + a few drops fish sauce (or omit for vegan).
  4. Toss cucumbers in the dressing. Pile in a shallow bowl.
  5. Spoon over chili crisp generously. Scatter sesame seeds + chopped coriander or scallion. Don't toss — the chili crisp should sit in a red slick on top.
