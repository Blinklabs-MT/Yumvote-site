Mexican Burrito Bowl

Rice, black beans, seasoned beef, salsa, guac, sour cream, cheese. All the burrito feeling, none of the wrapping fight.

Ingredients · per serving

  • ½ tsp Dried oregano
  • ½ lime Lime
  • ½ avocado Avocado
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tbsp Sour cream
  • 2 tbsp Salsa
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 70 g Long-grain rice
  • small handful Fresh coriander
  • 120 g Beef mince
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 120 g Tinned black beans
  • 30 g Grated cheese
  • 1 small onion Onion

Instructions

  1. Cook the rice: rinse, combine with 160ml water and pinch of salt. Bring to a boil, cover, lowest heat 15 min. Off heat 5 min still covered. Fluff.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat. Add the chopped onion. Cook 3 min. Add minced garlic, 30 sec.
  3. Add the beef mince. Break up with a spoon. Cook 6 min until browned all over and any liquid has evaporated.
  4. Add the smoked paprika, ground cumin, dried oregano, and salt. Stir 1 min — toasts spices.
  5. Add the drained black beans and 3 tbsp water. Simmer 3 min — flavours marry and beans heat through. Squeeze in lime juice. Taste — adjust salt.
  6. Mash the avocado in a bowl with a fork. Add a squeeze of lime, a pinch of salt, and a pinch of chopped coriander. That's the guac.
  7. Assemble: rice in the bowl. Beef + beans on top. Spoonful of salsa. Spoonful of guac. Dollop of sour cream. Sprinkle of cheese. Scatter coriander.
  8. Lime wedge on the side.
