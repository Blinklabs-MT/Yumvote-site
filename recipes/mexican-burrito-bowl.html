Mexican Burrito Bowl

30 minEasy~634 kcal / servingMexican
Contains dairygluten free

  • 2 tbsp Salsa
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 70 g Long-grain rice
  • small handful Fresh coriander
  • 120 g Beef mince
