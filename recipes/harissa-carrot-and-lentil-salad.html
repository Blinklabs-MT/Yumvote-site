Harissa Carrot & Lentil Salad

Harissa-roasted carrots over warm Puy lentils with feta, herbs and a pomegranate-yogurt drizzle. Middle Eastern vegetarian salad with serious flavour density.

40 minEasy~392 kcal / servingMiddle Eastern
Contains dairyContains nuts

Ingredients · per serving

  • 120 g Puy lentils
  • 1 leaf Bay leaf
  • 1 medium Lemon
  • 3 tbsp Fresh parsley
  • 6 medium Carrot
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 3 tbsp Pomegranate seeds
  • 4 tbsp Olive oil
  • 100 g Greek yogurt
  • 2 tbsp Harissa paste
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp Honey
  • 80 g Feta
  • 1 tbsp Pomegranate molasses
  • 30 g Walnut pieces
  • 2 tbsp Fresh mint
  • to taste Salt
