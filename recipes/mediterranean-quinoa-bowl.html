Mediterranean Quinoa Bowl

Fluffy lemon quinoa with cherry tomatoes, cucumber, olives, feta, chickpeas and a herby tahini drizzle.

25 minEasy~328 kcal / servingBright Plates

Ingredients · per serving

  • 80 g Cucumber
  • 1 lemon Lemon
  • small handful Fresh parsley
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 1 clove Garlic
  • 30 g Kalamata olives
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 80 g Quinoa
  • 2 tbsp Tahini
  • small handful Fresh mint
  • 100 g Cherry tomatoes
  • 120 g Tinned chickpeas
  • to taste Salt
  • 40 g Feta cheese
  • ½ tsp Smoked paprika

Instructions

  1. Rinse quinoa. Cook in salted water 12 min, drain, fluff. While warm, toss with lemon zest + juice + olive oil + pinch salt.
  2. Halve cherry tomatoes, cube cucumber, halve olives, crumble feta.
  3. Drain chickpeas, pat dry, toss with smoked paprika + olive oil + salt. Pan-fry 6 min until crisp at edges.
  4. Whisk tahini + lemon juice + minced garlic + warm water + salt + pepper into a pourable drizzle.
  5. Bowl: quinoa base → arrange tomato, cucumber, olives, crispy chickpeas, feta in separate piles. Top with mint + parsley.
  6. Drizzle tahini sauce over everything. Crack of pepper, extra lemon wedge on the side.
