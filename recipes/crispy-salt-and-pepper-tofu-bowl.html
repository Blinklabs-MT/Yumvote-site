Crispy Salt & Pepper Tofu Bowl

Cornflour-dusted tofu cubes fried until shatteringly crisp, tossed with garlic, chili and spring onion, over jasmine rice with stir-fried greens. Vegan crispy fakeaway, 30 minutes.

30 minEasy~821 kcal / servingChinese
Contains soyContains sesame

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 tsp Chili oil
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tsp Sea salt
  • 3 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • ½ tsp Sichuan peppercorns
  • 1 tbsp Sesame oil
  • 150 g Pak choi
  • 1 tsp White pepper
  • 1 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 60 g Cornflour
  • 300 g Firm tofu
  • 2 stalks Spring onion
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds
  • ½ tsp Five-spice powder
  • 75 g Jasmine rice
  • 1 small Red chili
