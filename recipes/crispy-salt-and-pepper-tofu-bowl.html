Crispy Salt & Pepper Tofu Bowl

Cornflour-dusted tofu cubes fried until shatteringly crisp, tossed with garlic, chili and spring onion, over jasmine rice with stir-fried greens. Vegan crispy fakeaway, 30 minutes.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 tsp Chili oil
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 3 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • ½ tsp Sichuan peppercorns
  • 1 tbsp Sesame oil
  • 150 g Pak choi
  • 1 tsp White pepper
  • 1 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 tsp Flaky sea salt
  • 60 g Cornflour
  • 300 g Firm tofu
  • 2 stalks Spring onion
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds
  • ½ tsp Five-spice powder
  • 75 g Jasmine rice
  • 1 small Red chili

Instructions

  1. Rinse the jasmine rice. Combine with 150ml water, pinch salt. Boil, cover, lowest heat 12 min, rest 5 min off heat.
  2. Drain the tofu, wrap in 4 sheets of kitchen paper, press firmly for 5 minutes. Cube into 2cm pieces.
  3. Make the salt & pepper mix: combine cornflour, sea salt, ground white pepper, Sichuan peppercorns (toasted and crushed) and Chinese five-spice in a wide bowl.
  4. Toss the tofu cubes in the salt & pepper mix until each is dusted dry-white.
  5. Heat 3 tbsp sunflower oil in a wide non-stick pan over medium-high heat. Fry the tofu 3 min per side (12 min total — 4 sides) until each face is deep gold and crisp on all surfaces. Lift onto kitchen paper.
  6. Wipe the pan. Add 1 tbsp sesame oil. Add the sliced garlic and red chili. Stir 30 seconds.
  7. Add the pak choi or bok choy with a splash of water. Cover 60 sec to wilt. Season with soy sauce.
  8. Build the bowls: rice as base. Top with the salt & pepper tofu and the greens. Scatter sliced spring onion, sesame seeds and an extra sprinkle of salt + pepper. Sliced chili and chili oil for those who want heat.
