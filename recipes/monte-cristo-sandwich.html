Monte Cristo Sandwich

Ham, turkey and Swiss cheese sandwich battered like French toast and pan-fried, dusted with icing sugar and served with jam for dipping. The American 1960s diner showstopper that's still iconic.

Ingredients · per serving

  • ½ medium Lemon
  • 1 medium Sweet potato
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 large Egg
  • 1 pinch Nutmeg
  • 80 g Cooked turkey
  • 4 tbsp Raspberry jam
  • 4 thick slices Brioche bread
  • 30 g Butter
  • 80 g Mixed salad leaves
  • 1 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 120 g Swiss cheese
  • 2 tbsp Icing sugar
  • to taste Salt
  • 80 g Cooked ham
  • 60 ml Whole milk

Instructions

  1. Lay out 4 slices of brioche bread. Smear Dijon mustard on 2 of the slices (these are the bottoms).
  2. Build each sandwich: bottom slice (with Dijon), 2 slices Swiss cheese, 2 slices ham, 2 slices turkey, 2 more slices Swiss cheese, top slice.
  3. Press the sandwiches firmly together with your palm.
  4. Make the batter: whisk eggs, milk, salt, pepper and a pinch of nutmeg in a wide shallow bowl.
  5. Heat 30g butter in a wide non-stick pan over medium-low heat.
  6. Dip each sandwich in the egg batter, turning to coat both sides AND the edges (let it soak briefly so the bread absorbs).
  7. Lay the sandwiches in the pan. Cook 3-4 min per side until deep gold and the cheese has melted (press gently with a spatula). Lower the heat if browning too fast.
  8. Lift onto warm plates. Dust generously with icing sugar through a small sieve. Plate with a ramekin of raspberry jam for dipping (yes, dip), and a side salad of mixed leaves tossed with olive oil and lemon. Sweet potato chips also alongside.
