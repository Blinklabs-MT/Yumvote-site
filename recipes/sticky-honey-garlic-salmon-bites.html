Sticky Honey Garlic Salmon Bites

Cubed salmon seared until crispy, glazed in a glossy honey-garlic-soy sauce, scattered with sesame and scallion.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 180 g Salmon fillet (skinless)
  • 1 tsp Toasted sesame seeds
  • to taste Black pepper
  • pinch Chilli flakes
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp Honey
  • 1.5 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • to taste Salt

Instructions

  1. Cube salmon into 2cm chunks. Pat very dry. Season with salt + pepper.
  2. Whisk honey + soy + minced garlic + rice vinegar + chilli flakes + 1 tsp sesame oil for the glaze.
  3. Heat 1 tbsp vegetable oil in a non-stick pan over medium-high. Sear salmon cubes 60 sec per side until golden. Don't over-crowd.
  4. Pour the glaze into the pan. Toss salmon to coat. Cook 60-90 sec until sauce thickens + clings + gets glossy.
  5. Tip into a bowl. Scatter sesame + scallion + a fresh chilli slice. Serve over rice or with steamed greens.
