Sticky Honey Garlic Salmon Bites

Cubed salmon seared until crispy, glazed in a glossy honey-garlic-soy sauce, scattered with sesame and scallion.

20 minEasy~503 kcal / servingSoul Food
Contains fishContains soyContains sesamedairy freepescatarian

Ingredients · per serving

  • 180 g Salmon fillet (skinless)
  • 1 tsp Toasted sesame seeds
  • to taste Black pepper
  • pinch Chilli flakes
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Vegetable oil
  • 1 tsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp Honey
  • 1.5 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
