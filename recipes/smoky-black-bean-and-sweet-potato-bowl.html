Smoky Black Bean & Sweet Potato Bowl

Roasted sweet potato cubes, smoky spiced black beans and lime rice with avocado, charred corn and a chipotle-cashew cream. Tex-Mex vegan bowl that satisfies way more than it looks.

Ingredients · per serving

  • ½ tsp Dried oregano
  • 1 medium Lime
  • ½ medium Avocado
  • 300 g Sweet potato
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 150 g Sweetcorn
  • 50 g Cashews
  • 2 tsp Ground cumin
  • 75 g Long-grain rice
  • 1 small bunch Fresh coriander
  • 1 tsp Chipotle paste
  • to taste Salt
  • 2 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 240 g Tinned black beans
  • ½ medium Onion

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C/200°C fan. Peel and cube the sweet potato into 2cm pieces. Toss with 1 tbsp olive oil, smoked paprika, ground cumin, salt and pepper on a baking tray.
  2. Roast 22-25 minutes, turning once, until tender with caramelised edges.
  3. Rinse the rice. Combine with 150ml water, pinch salt. Boil, cover, lowest heat 12 min, rest 5 min. Off heat, fold in lime zest and chopped coriander.
  4. Make the chipotle cashew cream: blitz the raw cashews (soaked 15 min in hot water if needed), chipotle paste, lime juice, garlic, salt and 4 tbsp water until silky. Add more water if too thick.
  5. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Add diced onion, cook 4 minutes until soft. Add minced garlic, ground cumin, smoked paprika, oregano. Stir 30 seconds.
  6. Drain and rinse the black beans. Tip into the pan with a splash of water. Cook 5 minutes, mashing some beans with the back of a spoon to thicken.
  7. Heat a dry pan over high heat. Add the sweetcorn, char 3-4 minutes until blistered. Tip into a bowl, toss with lime juice and chopped coriander.
  8. Build the bowls: lime rice as base. Pile on sweet potato, black beans, charred corn, sliced avocado. Drizzle chipotle cashew cream zigzags over the top. Scatter coriander, lime wedge.
