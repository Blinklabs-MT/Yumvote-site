Huevos Rancheros

Mexican breakfast-for-dinner: fried eggs on warm tortillas, blanketed in spiced tomato sauce, with cheese and beans on the side.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 2 small Corn tortillas
  • 1 clove Garlic
  • ¼ tsp Chilli powder
  • 1.5 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 large Egg
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 80 g Black beans
  • 150 g Chopped tomatoes (tinned)
  • small handful Fresh coriander
  • to taste Salt
  • 20 g Feta cheese
  • ½ tsp Smoked paprika
  • 30 g Onion

Instructions

  1. Make the sauce: heat 1 tsp oil in a small pan over medium heat. Add the finely chopped onion, cook 3 min until soft.
  2. Add the minced garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, chilli powder (if using). Stir 30 sec.
  3. Add the tinned chopped tomatoes. Simmer 8 min, mashing with a spoon until thickened. Season with salt.
  4. While the sauce simmers, warm the beans: drain and rinse the black beans. In a small pan, warm them with a splash of water and a pinch of salt and cumin. Mash a third with the back of a spoon to make them more saucy.
  5. Warm the tortillas: dry pan 20 sec per side. Place them on a serving plate.
  6. Fry the eggs: heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a non-stick frying pan over medium heat. Crack the eggs in carefully. Cook 2-3 min — the whites should be set but the yolks still runny (or cook longer if you prefer firm yolks).
  7. Lift each fried egg onto a warm tortilla.
  8. Spoon the warm tomato sauce around (not over) the eggs — you want the runny yolk visible. Sprinkle crumbled feta or grated cheese on top.
  9. Serve with the beans on the side and a sprinkle of fresh coriander.
