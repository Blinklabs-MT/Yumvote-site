Spicy Tuna Brown Rice Bowl

Diced sushi-grade tuna in a spicy mayo, piled on brown rice with cucumber, avocado, edamame and crispy nori. Modern poke-bowl variation with deeper nutty rice. 25 minutes.

25 minMedium~560 kcal / servingJapanese
Contains fishContains soyContains sesameContains eggs

Ingredients · per serving

  • ½ medium Cucumber
  • 3 tbsp Mayonnaise
  • 1 sheet Nori sheet
  • 1 medium Lime
  • ½ medium Avocado
  • 180 g Sushi-grade tuna steak
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Pickled ginger
  • 80 g Brown rice
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 80 g Edamame beans
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds
  • 1 tbsp Sriracha
