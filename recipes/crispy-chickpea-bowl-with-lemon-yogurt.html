Crispy Chickpea Bowl with Lemon Yogurt

Spice-roasted crispy chickpeas over jewelled rice with grilled halloumi, herb salad and a lemon-yogurt drizzle. Bright, fresh, modern Mediterranean.

30 minEasy~361 kcal / servingMediterranean
Contains dairy

Ingredients · per serving

  • ½ medium Cucumber
  • 1 medium Lemon
  • 3 tbsp Fresh parsley
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Fresh dill
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • 150 g Halloumi
  • 100 g Greek yogurt
  • ½ tsp Sumac
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp Fresh mint
  • 100 g Cherry tomatoes
  • 75 g Long-grain rice
  • 400 g Tinned chickpeas
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp Garlic powder
