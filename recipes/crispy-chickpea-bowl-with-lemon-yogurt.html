Crispy Chickpea Bowl with Lemon Yogurt

Spice-roasted crispy chickpeas over jewelled rice with grilled halloumi, herb salad and a lemon-yogurt drizzle. Bright, fresh, modern Mediterranean.

30 minEasy~361 kcal / servingMediterranean

Ingredients · per serving

  • ½ medium Cucumber
  • 1 medium Lemon
  • 3 tbsp Fresh parsley
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Fresh dill
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • 150 g Halloumi
  • 100 g Greek yogurt
  • ½ tsp Sumac
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 2 tbsp Fresh mint
  • 100 g Cherry tomatoes
  • 75 g Long-grain rice
  • 400 g Tinned chickpeas
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp Garlic powder

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C/200°C fan. Drain the chickpeas, pat completely dry. Toss with 1 tbsp olive oil, smoked paprika, ground cumin, garlic powder, salt and pepper.
  2. Spread on a baking tray, roast 22-25 min, shaking the tray once, until deeply crispy.
  3. Rinse the rice. Combine with 150ml water, pinch salt. Boil, cover, lowest heat 12 min, rest 5 min off heat. Stir in chopped parsley and a drizzle of olive oil.
  4. Mix the lemon yogurt: Greek yogurt, lemon juice, minced garlic, salt and pepper.
  5. Slice the halloumi into 1cm slabs. Pat dry. Heat 1 tsp olive oil in a non-stick pan over medium-high heat. Fry 90 sec per side until deep gold.
  6. Halve the cherry tomatoes. Tear the herbs (mint, parsley, dill). Slice the cucumber.
  7. Build the bowls: rice as base. Top with crispy chickpeas, grilled halloumi, cherry tomatoes, cucumber. Pile herbs on top. Drizzle lemon yogurt generously. Lemon wedge and a sprinkle of sumac.
