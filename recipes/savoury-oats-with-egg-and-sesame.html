Savoury Oats with Egg & Sesame

Creamy oats cooked with garlic, miso and parmesan, topped with a soft-boiled egg, scallion, chili oil and a runny yolk.

15 minEasy~344 kcal / servingBright Plates

Ingredients · per serving

  • drizzle Chili oil
  • 250 ml Vegetable stock
  • 1 tsp Toasted sesame seeds
  • 1 clove Garlic
  • 20 g Parmesan
  • 1 tsp Olive oil
  • 1 egg Egg
  • pinch Flaky sea salt
  • 60 g Porridge oats
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • 1 tsp White miso paste

Instructions

  1. Soft-boil egg: 6.5 min. Cold water, peel, halve.
  2. In a small pan, heat olive oil + minced garlic 30 sec.
  3. Add oats + stock + pinch salt. Cook 4 min creamy. Stir in miso + parmesan.
  4. Tip into wide bowl. Top with egg halves.
  5. Drizzle chili oil. Scatter scallion + sesame + flaky salt.
  6. Break egg over oats.
