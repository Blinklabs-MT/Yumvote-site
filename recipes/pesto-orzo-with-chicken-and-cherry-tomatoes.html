Pesto Orzo with Chicken & Cherry Tomatoes

Orzo pasta tossed in fresh basil pesto with shredded chicken, blistered cherry tomatoes and rocket. The 20-minute pasta-salad-bowl that tastes like a holiday lunch.

20 minEasy~412 kcal / servingItalian
Contains glutenContains dairyContains nuts

Ingredients · per serving

  • 200 g Orzo
  • ½ medium Lemon
  • 4 tbsp Basil pesto
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 60 g Rocket
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 200 g Cooked chicken breast
  • 60 g Feta
  • 200 g Cherry tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp Pine nuts
  • to taste Salt
  • to taste Fresh basil
