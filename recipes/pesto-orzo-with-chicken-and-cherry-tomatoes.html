Pesto Orzo with Chicken & Cherry Tomatoes

Orzo pasta tossed in fresh basil pesto with shredded chicken, blistered cherry tomatoes and rocket. The 20-minute pasta-salad-bowl that tastes like a holiday lunch.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 200 g Orzo
  • ½ medium Lemon
  • 4 tbsp Basil pesto
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 60 g Rocket
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 200 g Cooked chicken breast
  • 60 g Feta
  • 200 g Cherry tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp Pine nuts
  • to taste Salt
  • to taste Fresh basil

Instructions

  1. Bring a pot of well-salted water to the boil. Cook the orzo to packet time.
  2. Shred the chicken (use cooked rotisserie or leftover roast chicken).
  3. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat. Add the cherry tomatoes and minced garlic. Cook 4-5 minutes, tossing, until the tomatoes burst.
  4. Add the shredded chicken to the pan, warm through 60 seconds.
  5. Drain the orzo, reserving 4 tbsp pasta water. Tip the orzo back in the pot.
  6. Add the pesto and a splash of pasta water to the orzo. Toss to coat.
  7. Tip in the burst-tomato chicken mixture, fold gently.
  8. Add a handful of rocket leaves and torn basil — they wilt slightly in the heat. Plate, crumble feta, scatter pine nuts, drizzle olive oil. Lemon wedge.
