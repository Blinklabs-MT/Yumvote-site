Tuna Melt

Toasted bread topped with creamy tuna salad and melted cheddar. Diner counter classic — quick, satisfying, cheap.

15 minEasy~551 kcal / servingAmerican

Ingredients · per serving

  • 2 tbsp Mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Lemon juice
  • 20 g Celery
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 slices Bread
  • small handful Fresh dill
  • 15 g Red onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 2 slices Cheddar cheese
  • 80 g Tinned tuna

Instructions

  1. Drain the tinned tuna well, pressing with the back of a spoon to squeeze out water/oil.
  2. In a bowl, flake the tuna with a fork. Add the mayonnaise, finely chopped celery, finely chopped red onion, lemon juice, dill (if using), a pinch of salt, and a few grinds of pepper. Mix well.
  3. Heat your oven grill (broiler) to high. Or heat a dry frying pan over medium heat for a stovetop version.
  4. Toast one side of the bread slices: under the grill 1-2 min, OR in the dry pan 2 min, until golden.
  5. Flip the bread. Spread the tuna mixture evenly on the un-toasted side.
  6. Top with the cheddar cheese slices.
  7. Return under the grill or back to the pan (cover with a lid for the pan method). Cook 2-3 min until the cheese is bubbling and golden.
  8. Slice in half on the diagonal. Serve with a few pickles or crisps on the side.
