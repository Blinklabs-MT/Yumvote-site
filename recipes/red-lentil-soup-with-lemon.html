Red Lentil Soup with Lemon

Red lentils simmered with carrots, cumin, and a hit of lemon. Cheap, fast, filling, healthy.

30 minEasy~322 kcal / servingMediterranean

Ingredients · per serving

  • 600 ml Vegetable stock
  • ½ lemon Lemon
  • small handful Fresh parsley
  • 100 g Carrot
  • to taste Black pepper
  • pinch Chilli flakes
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tsp Ground coriander
  • 1.5 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp Tomato paste
  • to taste Salt
  • 100 g Red lentils (split)
  • 1 onion Onion

Instructions

  1. Rinse the red lentils in a sieve under cold water until the water runs clear (1 min). Removes starch and any dust.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a pot over medium heat. Add the chopped onion. Cook 5 min until soft.
  3. Add the minced garlic, grated carrot, ground cumin, ground coriander, and chilli flakes (if using). Stir 1 min — toasts the spices.
  4. Add the rinsed lentils, vegetable stock, and tomato paste. Stir.
  5. Bring to a boil, then lower to a simmer. Cover. Cook 18 min — lentils should be soft and starting to fall apart.
  6. Blend smooth with a stick blender (or in a regular blender). Add a splash more water if too thick.
  7. Stir in the lemon juice. Taste — adjust salt, lemon, and pepper. Should taste bright.
  8. Serve in bowls drizzled with extra olive oil, chopped parsley, and a wedge of lemon. Warm bread on the side.
