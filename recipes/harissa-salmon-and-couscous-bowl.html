Harissa Salmon & Couscous Bowl

Salmon fillets glazed with harissa-honey, pan-seared, served on fluffy couscous with roasted aubergine, chickpeas and lemon-yogurt sauce. North African-Mediterranean bowl, bold and bright.

25 minEasy~535 kcal / servingMediterranean
Contains fishContains glutenContains dairy

Ingredients · per serving

  • 250 g Salmon fillet (skinless)
  • 150 ml Vegetable stock
  • 1 medium Lemon
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 1 clove Garlic
  • 2 tbsp Pomegranate seeds
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • 100 g Greek yogurt
  • 80 g Couscous
  • 1 tbsp Harissa paste
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp Honey
  • 2 tbsp Fresh mint
  • 240 g Tinned chickpeas
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 medium Aubergine
