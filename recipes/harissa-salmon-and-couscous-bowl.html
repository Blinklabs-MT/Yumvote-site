Harissa Salmon & Couscous Bowl

Salmon fillets glazed with harissa-honey, pan-seared, served on fluffy couscous with roasted aubergine, chickpeas and lemon-yogurt sauce. North African-Mediterranean bowl, bold and bright.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 250 g Salmon fillet (skinless)
  • 150 ml Vegetable stock
  • 1 medium Lemon
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 1 clove Garlic
  • 2 tbsp Pomegranate seeds
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • 100 g Greek yogurt
  • 80 g Couscous
  • 1 tbsp Harissa paste
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp Honey
  • 2 tbsp Fresh mint
  • 240 g Tinned chickpeas
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 medium Aubergine

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C/200°C fan. Cube the aubergine into 2cm pieces. Drain the chickpeas. Toss aubergine and chickpeas on a baking tray with 2 tbsp olive oil, ground cumin, salt and pepper.
  2. Roast 18-20 minutes until the aubergine is tender and the chickpeas start to crisp.
  3. While they roast, put the couscous in a heatproof bowl. Pour over the boiling stock, cover with a plate, leave 5 minutes. Fluff with a fork, stir in chopped parsley and a drizzle of olive oil.
  4. Mix the glaze: harissa paste, honey, lemon juice and a pinch of salt.
  5. Pat the salmon fillets dry. Brush generously with the glaze on both sides.
  6. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat. Lay the salmon skin-side down. Cook 4 minutes — skin crisp. Flip, 2 minutes more, basting with extra glaze. The salmon should flake at the centre.
  7. Mix the yogurt sauce: Greek yogurt, lemon juice, minced garlic, chopped mint and pinch of salt.
  8. Build the bowls: couscous as base. Top with the salmon, roasted aubergine and chickpeas. Dollop yogurt sauce. Scatter pomegranate seeds, fresh mint and a lemon wedge.
