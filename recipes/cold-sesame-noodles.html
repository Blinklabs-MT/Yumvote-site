Cold Sesame Noodles

Chilled noodles tossed in a creamy peanut-sesame-soy dressing with cucumber ribbons, scallion and crushed peanuts.

15 minEasy~382 kcal / servingBright Plates

Ingredients · per serving

  • 120 g Cucumber
  • drizzle Chili oil
  • 1 lime Lime
  • 1 tsp Toasted sesame seeds
  • 120 g Wheat noodles
  • 1 clove Garlic
  • 1 tsp Honey or maple
  • 2 tbsp Peanut butter
  • 1.5 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp Fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Tahini
  • small handful Fresh coriander
  • 2 stalks Spring onion
  • 30 g Peanuts

Instructions

  1. Cook noodles to packet. Drain, rinse cold to stop cooking.
  2. Whisk: peanut butter + tahini + soy + rice vinegar + sesame oil + ginger + garlic + honey + lime + chili oil + 3 tbsp warm water until creamy.
  3. Slice cucumber ribbons with peeler. Slice scallion. Crush peanuts.
  4. Tip noodles into wide bowl. Half dressing over. Toss until every strand coated.
  5. Plate. Top with cucumber, scallion, peanuts, coriander, sesame, chili oil.
  6. Cold. Extra dressing on side.
