Crispy Salmon Rice Bowl with Slaw

Salmon pieces tossed in cornflour and pan-fried crispy, glazed with a sticky soy-ginger sauce, served over rice with crunchy slaw, avocado and toasted sesame. 25 minutes of texture.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 100 g Red cabbage
  • 250 g Salmon fillet (skinless)
  • ½ medium Lime
  • ½ medium Avocado
  • 1 medium Carrot
  • to taste Black pepper
  • ¼ tsp Chili flakes
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 3 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • ½ tsp Caster sugar
  • 2 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp Fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp Honey
  • 3 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp Cornflour
  • 2 tbsp Fresh coriander
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds
  • 75 g Jasmine rice

Instructions

  1. Rinse the rice. Combine with 150ml water, pinch salt. Boil, cover, lowest heat 12 min, rest 5 min off heat.
  2. Cube the salmon (skinless) into 2-3cm pieces. Toss with cornflour, salt and pepper until each piece is dusted dry-white.
  3. Make the slaw: shred the red cabbage and carrot finely. Toss with rice vinegar, sesame oil, soy sauce, pinch sugar and chopped coriander.
  4. Mix the glaze: soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, minced garlic, grated ginger and chili flakes (if using) in a small bowl.
  5. Heat 3 tbsp sunflower oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat. Add the salmon pieces in a single layer (don't crowd). Fry 2 minutes per side until deeply golden and crisp.
  6. Pour off most of the oil. Pour the glaze into the pan. Toss the salmon to coat — it'll bubble and thicken in 30 seconds. Off the heat immediately so the crust stays crispy.
  7. Build the bowls: rice as base. Pile on the glazed salmon, slaw and sliced avocado. Scatter sesame seeds, sliced spring onion and a wedge of lime.
