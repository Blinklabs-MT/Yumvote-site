Miso Glazed Aubergine with Rice

Halved aubergines scored, brushed with a sweet-savoury miso-mirin glaze, roasted until tender and lacquered. Served over jasmine rice with steamed greens. Japanese vegan elegance, 35 minutes.

35 minEasy~393 kcal / servingJapanese
Contains soyContains sesame

Ingredients · per serving

  • 2 tbsp Mirin
  • ¼ tsp Chili flakes
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp Fresh ginger
  • 2 tbsp Sesame oil
  • 150 g Pak choi
  • 1 tbsp Honey
  • 1 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 2 medium Aubergine
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds
  • 75 g Jasmine rice
  • 3 tbsp White miso paste
