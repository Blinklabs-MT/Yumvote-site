Mince & Tatties

Scottish midweek comfort — beef mince cooked down with onions, carrots and gravy, served over creamy mashed potatoes. The dinner kids ask for after a long school day.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 350 ml Beef stock
  • 100 g Frozen peas
  • 1 tbsp Plain flour
  • 500 g Maris Piper potatoes
  • 2 medium Carrot
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 1 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • 30 g Butter
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 250 g Beef mince
  • to taste Salt
  • ½ tsp Marmite
  • 80 ml Whole milk
  • 1 medium Onion

Instructions

  1. Peel and chunk the potatoes. Put on to boil in salted water — they'll take 18 minutes until knife-tender.
  2. While they boil, finely dice the onion and carrot. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat. Add the mince. Brown hard for 6-8 minutes, breaking up with a wooden spoon, until you have crispy bits and the fat has rendered.
  3. Push the meat to one side. Add the diced veg to the empty space, cook 5 minutes until softened.
  4. Stir in the flour, cook 1 minute. Add the beef stock, marmite and Worcestershire. Stir to dissolve any flour lumps.
  5. Bring to a simmer. Cover loosely, simmer 15 minutes until the gravy is glossy and the mince is rich. Add the frozen peas in the last 2 minutes.
  6. Drain the potatoes well. Mash with butter, warm milk, salt and pepper until smooth.
  7. Plate the mash, make a dent with the back of a spoon, ladle the mince and gravy generously into the well. Eat hot.
