Mince & Tatties

Scottish midweek comfort — beef mince cooked down with onions, carrots and gravy, served over creamy mashed potatoes. The dinner kids ask for after a long school day.

40 minEasy~496 kcal / servingBritish
Contains glutenContains dairy

Ingredients · per serving

  • 350 ml Beef stock
  • 100 g Frozen peas
  • 1 tbsp Plain flour
  • 500 g Maris Piper potatoes
  • 2 medium Carrot
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 1 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • 30 g Butter
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 250 g Beef mince
  • to taste Salt
  • ½ tsp Marmite
  • 80 ml Whole milk
  • 1 medium Onion
