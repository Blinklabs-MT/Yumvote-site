Sticky Chicken Drumsticks with Roast Veg

Chicken drumsticks coated in a sticky honey-soy-garlic glaze, roasted with carrots, parsnips and onion until the chicken is fall-off-the-bone and the veg is caramelised. One tray, hand-eating, kid heaven.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 2 medium Parsnip
  • 3 medium Carrot
  • to taste Black pepper
  • ¼ tsp Chili flakes
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Fresh ginger
  • 3 tbsp Honey
  • 3 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 medium Red onion
  • 6 drumsticks Chicken drumsticks
  • 2 stalks Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Fresh thyme
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds
  • 2 tbsp Tomato ketchup

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C/200°C fan. Cut the carrots, parsnips and red onion into 2-3cm chunks.
  2. Tip the veg onto a large roasting tray. Drizzle with 2 tbsp olive oil, salt, pepper and thyme. Toss.
  3. Score the chicken drumsticks (3-4 shallow cuts each so the glaze penetrates). Pat dry.
  4. Mix the glaze: honey, soy sauce, ketchup, minced garlic, grated ginger and a pinch of chili flakes (skip for kids).
  5. Add the drumsticks to the veg tray. Brush them generously with half the glaze. Roast 40-45 minutes, basting with the remaining glaze every 15 minutes.
  6. The chicken is done when the juices run clear and the skin is deep mahogany and sticky. The veg should be tender with caramelised edges.
  7. Lift onto a warm platter. Scatter chopped spring onion and sesame seeds. Serve with rice or crusty bread to mop up the pan juices.
