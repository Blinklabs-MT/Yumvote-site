Tortellini in Tomato Cream Sauce

Fresh cheese tortellini tossed in a creamy tomato sauce with garlic and basil, topped with parmesan. The 15-minute pasta that kids will ask for next week. Shop-bought fresh tortellini, no pasta-making required.

15 minEasy~497 kcal / servingItalian
Contains glutenContains dairyContains eggs

Ingredients · per serving

  • ½ tsp Dried oregano
  • 250 g Fresh cheese tortellini
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 40 g Parmesan
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 pinch Caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp Tomato purée
  • to taste Salt
  • to taste Fresh basil
  • 300 g Passata
  • 100 ml Double cream
