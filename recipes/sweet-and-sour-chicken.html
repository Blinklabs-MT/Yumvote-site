Sweet & Sour Chicken

Crispy battered chicken pieces tossed in a glossy sweet-and-sour sauce with peppers and pineapple, served over jasmine rice. Cantonese takeaway favourite, made better at home in 30 minutes.

30 minMedium~544 kcal / servingChinese
Contains soyContains sesame

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 medium Red bell pepper
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 200 g Chicken thigh fillets
  • 100 ml Sunflower oil
  • 3 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp Fresh ginger
  • 2 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp Cornflour
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds
  • 3 tbsp Tomato ketchup
  • 2 tbsp Light brown sugar
  • 100 g Pineapple chunks
  • 75 g Jasmine rice
  • ½ medium Onion
