Lemon Chicken Salad with Quinoa

Lemon-herb grilled chicken sliced over quinoa with avocado, cucumber, cherry tomatoes, feta and a tahini-lemon dressing. Modern protein-packed salad, lunch-or-dinner.

Ingredients · per serving

  • ½ medium Cucumber
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 2 medium Lemon
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley
  • ½ medium Avocado
  • 250 g Chicken breast
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 2 tbsp Pomegranate seeds
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • 75 g Quinoa
  • 60 g Feta
  • 1 tbsp Sliced almonds
  • 2 tbsp Tahini
  • 2 tbsp Fresh mint
  • 100 g Cherry tomatoes
  • to taste Salt

Instructions

  1. Rinse the quinoa. Combine with 150ml water, pinch salt. Boil, cover, lowest heat 12 min, rest 5 min off heat.
  2. Pat the chicken breasts dry. Marinate: olive oil, lemon juice, lemon zest, minced garlic, dried oregano, salt, pepper.
  3. Heat a griddle pan over high heat, brush with oil. Lay the chicken down. Cook 4 min per side until cooked through with charred lines. Lift onto a board, rest 5 min, slice on the diagonal.
  4. Make the tahini dressing: whisk tahini, lemon juice, minced garlic, salt and 3 tbsp warm water until smooth.
  5. Halve the cherry tomatoes. Slice the cucumber. Cube the avocado.
  6. Tip the quinoa into a large bowl. Fold in chopped mint and parsley.
  7. Build the salads: quinoa as base. Arrange the chicken, cucumber, tomatoes, avocado in piles. Crumble feta over. Drizzle generously with tahini dressing. Scatter pomegranate seeds, more mint, sliced almonds and a lemon wedge.
