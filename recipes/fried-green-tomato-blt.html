Fried Green Tomato BLT

Cornmeal-fried green tomatoes layered with crispy bacon, lettuce and remoulade in a brioche bun. Southern American twist on the BLT — tangy green tomato sings with smoky bacon.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 80 ml Buttermilk
  • ¼ medium Cucumber
  • ½ medium Lemon
  • 4 tbsp Mayonnaise
  • 80 g Plain flour
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 3 medium Green tomatoes
  • 2 tsp Cajun seasoning
  • 250 ml Sunflower oil
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 large Egg
  • 4 leaves Little gem lettuce
  • 1 tsp Hot sauce
  • 120 g Smoked bacon
  • 1 tsp Balsamic vinegar
  • 60 g Cornmeal
  • 60 g Cherry tomatoes
  • 60 g Mixed salad leaves
  • ½ tsp Dijon mustard
  • to taste Salt
  • 2 small Gherkins
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 2 buns Brioche bun
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder

Instructions

  1. Dice the bacon. Crisp in a dry wide pan over medium heat 5-6 min until golden. Lift onto kitchen paper.
  2. Slice the green tomatoes 8mm thick. Season generously with salt and pepper.
  3. Set up dredging: bowl 1 = plain flour with smoked paprika and garlic powder. Bowl 2 = beaten egg + buttermilk. Bowl 3 = cornmeal + plain flour + Cajun seasoning + salt + pepper.
  4. Dredge each tomato slice: flour first, then egg-buttermilk, then cornmeal (press firmly).
  5. Heat 1cm of sunflower oil in a wide pan to 175°C. Fry the tomato slices in 2 batches for 3-4 min per side until deep gold and crispy. Lift onto a wire rack.
  6. Make the remoulade: mayonnaise, Dijon mustard, hot sauce, lemon juice, smoked paprika, finely diced gherkin and chopped parsley.
  7. Lightly toast the brioche buns. Build: bun base, smear of remoulade, lettuce leaves, slices of crispy bacon, 2-3 fried green tomato slices, more remoulade, bun lid.
  8. Plate with a small side salad of leaves, tomato and cucumber, dressed in olive oil + balsamic. Crisps or oven-baked fries alongside.
