Salmon Spinach Pasta

Pan-seared salmon flaked through creamy lemon-spinach pasta with garlic, parmesan and pine nuts. Italian elegance in 25 minutes — feels luxurious for very little effort.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 250 g Salmon fillet (skinless)
  • 1 medium Lemon
  • 150 g Crème fraîche
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 40 g Parmesan
  • 3 tbsp Fresh dill
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 200 g Linguine
  • 1 pinch Nutmeg
  • 20 g Butter
  • 1 tbsp Pine nuts
  • to taste Salt
  • 100 g Baby spinach

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a rolling boil. Cook the linguine to packet time.
  2. Pat the salmon dry. Season with salt and pepper. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat. Lay salmon skin-side down. Cook 4 min until crispy. Flip, 2 min more. Lift onto a plate.
  3. Lower heat to medium. Add the butter to the pan, then minced garlic. Stir 30 sec.
  4. Add the crème fraîche, lemon zest, lemon juice and a splash of pasta water. Whisk to combine.
  5. Add the spinach in handfuls, stirring as it wilts (60 seconds).
  6. Off the heat, stir in the grated parmesan and most of the dill. Season with salt, pepper and a pinch of nutmeg.
  7. Drain the pasta, reserving a mug of water. Tip into the sauce with a splash of pasta water. Toss vigorously.
  8. Flake the salmon (discarding skin if you prefer) and fold gently through the pasta. Plate, scatter pine nuts, more dill, lemon zest and a final grating of parmesan.
