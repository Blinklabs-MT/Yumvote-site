White Fish with Lemon Caper Butter

Pan-fried cod fillets with crispy skin, finished in a lemon-caper butter sauce, served with smashed minty peas and new potatoes. British coastal classic, restaurant-ready in 25 minutes.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 medium Lemon
  • 250 g Frozen peas
  • 3 tbsp Fresh parsley
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Fresh dill
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 60 g Butter
  • 3 tbsp Fresh mint
  • to taste Salt
  • 2 tbsp Capers
  • 300 g Cod fillet
  • 300 g New potatoes

Instructions

  1. Halve the new potatoes. Boil in salted water 12-15 min until knife-tender. Drain.
  2. Cook the frozen peas in boiling water for 3 min. Drain. Tip into a bowl with 20g of the butter, chopped mint, lemon zest, salt and pepper. Smash with a fork — leave some peas whole for texture.
  3. Pat the fish fillets very dry. Season the skin generously with salt and pepper.
  4. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a non-stick pan over medium-high heat. Lay the fish skin-side down. Press lightly with a fish slice for 30 seconds (stops curling). Don't move for 3-4 minutes — skin should be deeply golden and crisp.
  5. Flip carefully, 90 seconds more. Lift onto warm plates.
  6. Wipe the pan. Add the remaining butter, melt over medium heat. When foaming, add the capers and minced garlic. Stir 30 seconds.
  7. Squeeze in the lemon juice. Stir in chopped parsley. The sauce should be glossy and emulsified.
  8. Plate the smashed peas. Lay fish on top. Spoon the lemon-caper butter generously over the fish. Boiled potatoes alongside, a sprinkle of dill and a lemon wedge.
