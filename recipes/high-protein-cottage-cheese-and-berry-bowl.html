High-Protein Cottage Cheese & Berry Bowl

Cottage cheese topped with berries, honey, nuts, and seeds. Late-evening protein hit, breakfast-for-dinner, or pre-bed slow protein.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 2 tbsp Granola
  • 100 g Mixed berries
  • 200 g Cottage cheese
  • 1 tbsp Mixed seeds
  • 1 tbsp Chopped almonds
  • pinch Ground cinnamon
  • 1 tsp Honey or maple syrup

Instructions

  1. Spoon the cottage cheese into a bowl.
  2. Top with mixed berries — frozen are fine (let them thaw 5 min, releases lovely syrup) or fresh.
  3. Drizzle honey or maple syrup.
  4. Scatter granola, mixed seeds, and chopped almonds.
  5. A little cinnamon dusted on top. Eat.
  6. Variation 1 (savoury): cottage cheese + sliced cucumber + tomato + dill + olive oil + salt + pepper + sourdough toast.
  7. Variation 2 (sweet evening): add half a sliced banana + 1 tsp peanut butter for extra slow protein and richness.
