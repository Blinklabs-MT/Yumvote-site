Grilled Tofu with Mango Salsa

Tofu slabs marinated in soy-ginger and grilled until charred, served over coconut rice with a fresh mango-chili-lime salsa. Caribbean-Asian fusion vegan dinner with serious zing.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 2 medium Lime
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tsp Sunflower oil
  • 1 medium Ripe mango
  • 70 ml Coconut milk
  • 2 tsp Fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 3 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 small bunch Fresh coriander
  • ¼ medium Red onion
  • 300 g Firm tofu
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tbsp Light brown sugar
  • 75 g Jasmine rice
  • 2 tbsp Toasted coconut flakes
  • ½ small Red chili

Instructions

  1. Drain the tofu, wrap in 4 sheets of kitchen paper, press firmly for 5 min. Slice into 1cm slabs.
  2. Marinate the tofu: combine soy sauce, lime juice, brown sugar, grated ginger, minced garlic and sesame oil. Pour over the tofu, let sit 10 min.
  3. Rinse the jasmine rice. Combine with 130ml water + 70ml coconut milk and a pinch of salt. Boil, cover, lowest heat 12 min, rest 5 min off heat.
  4. Make the mango salsa: dice the mango small. Mix with diced red onion, chopped red chili, lime juice, chopped coriander and a pinch of salt.
  5. Heat a griddle pan or wide pan over high heat, brush with oil. Lay the marinated tofu down. Cook 3-4 min per side until charred and heated through.
  6. Fluff the coconut rice with a fork, stir in lime zest.
  7. Plate the coconut rice. Lay the grilled tofu on top. Spoon the mango salsa generously over. Scatter coriander, toasted coconut flakes and a lime wedge.
