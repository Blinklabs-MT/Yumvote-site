Panang Beef Curry

Thai panang curry — beef strips simmered in a thick, peanutty red curry with kaffir lime, basil and coconut milk. Smoother and richer than Thai red curry, ready in 25 minutes.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 3 leaves Kaffir lime leaves
  • 1 medium Lime
  • 1 medium Red bell pepper
  • 1 tbsp Fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp Roasted peanuts (unsalted)
  • 1 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • 1 small bunch Thai basil
  • 2 tbsp Peanut butter
  • 200 g Beef sirloin
  • 250 ml Coconut milk
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Light brown sugar
  • 75 g Jasmine rice
  • ½ small Red chili
  • 2 tbsp Panang curry paste

Instructions

  1. Rinse the rice. Combine with 150ml water, pinch salt. Boil, cover, lowest heat 12 min, rest 5 min off heat.
  2. Slice the beef against the grain into thin strips, about 5mm thick.
  3. Heat 1 tbsp sunflower oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat. Add the panang curry paste. Stir 90 seconds until fragrant and the oil splits red.
  4. Pour in half the coconut milk and the peanut butter. Whisk to combine. Bubble 2 minutes — the oil should rise.
  5. Add the beef strips, stir to coat, cook 2 minutes until just sealed.
  6. Add the remaining coconut milk, fish sauce, brown sugar, lime leaves and sliced red pepper. Lower heat, simmer 6-8 minutes until the sauce is thick and the beef is tender.
  7. Tear in Thai basil leaves. Squeeze in lime juice. Taste — adjust salt with fish sauce, sweetness with sugar. Spoon over the rice, scatter chopped peanuts and sliced chili.
