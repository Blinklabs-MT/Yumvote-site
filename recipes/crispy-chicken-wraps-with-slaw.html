Crispy Chicken Wraps with Slaw

Crispy breaded chicken strips in warm tortillas with crunchy slaw, melted cheese and a smoky-garlic mayo. The fakeaway wrap that kids race to assemble themselves.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 4 large Flour tortilla
  • 150 g Red cabbage
  • ½ medium Lemon
  • 6 tbsp Mayonnaise
  • 30 g Plain flour
  • 300 g Chicken breast
  • 1 medium Carrot
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 1 clove Garlic
  • 30 g Parmesan
  • 3 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • 1 tbsp Cider vinegar
  • 1 large Egg
  • ½ tsp Caster sugar
  • 50 g Mature cheddar
  • 80 g Panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 tbsp Fresh chives
  • to taste Salt
  • 1.5 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder

Instructions

  1. Slice the chicken breast horizontally to make 2 thin pieces. Pound to 5mm thick. Cut into 1cm strips.
  2. Set up 3 plates: seasoned flour (with smoked paprika, garlic powder, salt and pepper), beaten egg, panko mixed with grated parmesan.
  3. Dredge each chicken strip: flour, egg, panko-parmesan. Press the coating on firmly.
  4. Heat 3 tbsp sunflower oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat. Fry the strips 2-3 minutes per side until deep golden and crisp. Lift onto kitchen paper.
  5. Make the slaw: shred the red cabbage and carrot finely. Toss with mayonnaise, cider vinegar, sugar, salt and chopped chives.
  6. Make the smoky mayo: mix mayonnaise, smoked paprika, minced garlic, lemon juice, hot sauce (optional), salt.
  7. Warm the tortillas in a dry pan 20 seconds per side. Stack and wrap in a tea towel.
  8. Build: warm tortilla, smear of smoky mayo, pile of slaw, 2-3 chicken strips, scatter cheese, more mayo. Fold the bottom up, then the sides, then roll tight. Slice in half on the diagonal.
