Black Bean Tacos

Smoky spiced black beans piled into warm tortillas with crunchy slaw, avocado, and lime. Vegan tacos that actually fill you up.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 60 g Red cabbage
  • ½ tsp Dried oregano
  • 1 lime Lime
  • 3 small Corn tortillas
  • ½ avocado Avocado
  • 1 clove Garlic
  • ¼ tsp Chilli powder
  • 1.5 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 150 g Black beans
  • small handful Fresh coriander
  • to taste Salt
  • ½ tsp Smoked paprika
  • 40 g Onion

Instructions

  1. Drain and rinse the black beans.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a frying pan over medium heat. Add the chopped onion, cook 3 min until soft.
  3. Add the minced garlic, cumin, smoked paprika, oregano, and chilli powder (if using). Stir 30 seconds.
  4. Add the black beans + 2 tbsp water. Cook 3 min, mashing about half the beans with the back of a spoon to make a chunky-creamy texture.
  5. Add lime juice (1 tsp) and salt to taste. Off the heat.
  6. Make a quick slaw: in a small bowl, toss the shredded red cabbage with a pinch of salt, 1 tsp lime juice, and a drizzle of olive oil. Squeeze with your hands for 30 sec — wilts slightly and softens.
  7. Slice the avocado just before serving (it browns quickly).
  8. Warm the tortillas: dry pan 20 sec per side.
  9. Build the tacos: smear black beans on each warm tortilla, top with red cabbage slaw, sliced avocado, fresh coriander, and a final squeeze of lime.
