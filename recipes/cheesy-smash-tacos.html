Cheesy Smash Tacos

Beef mince smashed onto warm corn tortillas with cheese, charred crispy, folded and topped with onion, coriander and lime. TikTok's most-tried recipe, ready in 20 minutes.

20 minEasy~620 kcal / servingMexican
Contains dairy

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 medium Lime
  • 6 small Corn tortillas
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 1 tsp Onion powder
  • 60 ml Sour cream
  • to taste Hot sauce
  • 100 g Mature cheddar
  • 2 tsp Ground cumin
  • ½ medium White onion
  • 1 small bunch Fresh coriander
  • 300 g Beef mince
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
