Vegan Black Bean Chilli

Smoky, deeply spiced black bean chilli with peppers, sweetcorn, and a hint of dark chocolate. All the comfort, none of the beef.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 150 ml Vegetable stock
  • ½ lime Lime
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 1.5 tbsp Olive oil
  • 80 g Sweetcorn
  • 1 pepper Red pepper
  • 1.5 tsp Ground cumin
  • 10 g Dark chocolate (70%)
  • small handful Fresh coriander
  • to taste Chipotle paste
  • 1 tbsp Tomato paste
  • 1 onion Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 250 g Tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 240 g Tinned black beans
  • pinch Sugar
  • 1 onion Onion

Instructions

  1. Heat 1.5 tbsp olive oil in a pot over medium heat. Add chopped onion, diced red pepper. Cook 6 min until soft.
  2. Add minced garlic, ground cumin, smoked paprika, dried oregano, and chipotle paste (if using). Stir 1 min — toasts spices.
  3. Add the tomato paste. Stir 1 min — turns brick red.
  4. Pour in the tinned tomatoes. Stir, scrape any browned bits off the bottom of the pot.
  5. Add the drained black beans, sweetcorn, vegetable stock, and a pinch of salt.
  6. Bring to a simmer. Lower heat. Cook uncovered 20 min, stirring every 5 min, until thick and rich.
  7. Off the heat, stir in the dark chocolate (yes — it adds depth, you won't taste chocolate) and lime juice. Stir until chocolate melts.
  8. Taste — adjust salt, lime, and a pinch of sugar if it tastes too sharp.
  9. Serve over rice or with tortilla chips. Top with chopped coriander, sliced spring onion, a wedge of lime, and (if not strictly vegan) a dollop of sour cream + grated cheese.
