Sweet Potato & Chickpea Buddha Bowl

Roasted sweet potato, crispy chickpeas, kale and quinoa with a creamy tahini-lemon dressing, finished with pomegranate and pumpkin seeds. The vegan bowl that doesn't feel like settling.

35 minEasy~562 kcal / servingMediterranean
Contains sesame

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 medium Lemon
  • 250 g Sweet potato
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 1 clove Garlic
  • 2 tbsp Pomegranate seeds
  • 1 tsp Maple syrup
  • 80 g Kale
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 75 g Quinoa
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp Pumpkin seeds
  • 2 tbsp Tahini
  • 240 g Tinned chickpeas
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp Garlic powder
