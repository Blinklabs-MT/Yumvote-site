Red Beans & Rice

Slow-cooked red kidney beans with andouille sausage, the Cajun holy trinity (onion-celery-pepper), garlic and bay, ladled over fluffy long-grain rice. New Orleans Monday tradition.

120 minMedium~684 kcal / servingAmerican

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1200 ml Chicken stock
  • 1 large Green bell pepper
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 2 leaves Bay leaf
  • 2 stalks Celery
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 5 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • to taste Hot sauce
  • ½ tsp Cayenne pepper
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 400 g Dried red kidney beans
  • 150 g Long-grain rice
  • 1 tsp Dried thyme
  • 2 stalks Spring onion
  • 200 g Chorizo
  • to taste Salt
  • 2 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 large Onion
