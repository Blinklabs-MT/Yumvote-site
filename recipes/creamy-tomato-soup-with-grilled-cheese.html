Creamy Tomato Soup with Grilled Cheese

Velvet-smooth tomato soup with a touch of cream and a grilled cheese sandwich for dunking. The classic pairing.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 250 ml Vegetable stock
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 2 slices Bread
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • ½ tsp Dried basil
  • 15 g Butter
  • to taste Salt
  • 60 g Cheddar cheese
  • few leaves Fresh basil
  • 400 g Tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 50 ml Double cream
  • 1 tsp Sugar
  • 1 onion Onion

Instructions

  1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil + 15g butter in a pot over medium heat. Add the chopped onion. Cook 6 min until soft (not browned — softer = sweeter soup).
  2. Add the minced garlic, sugar, and dried basil. Stir 30 sec.
  3. Pour in the tinned tomatoes (juice and all) and the stock. Add a pinch of salt. Bring to a simmer. Cover. Cook 15 min.
  4. Blend smooth — either with a stick blender straight in the pot, or carefully in a regular blender (fill no more than half; vent the lid).
  5. Return to the pot if blended elsewhere. Stir in the double cream. Taste — adjust salt, pepper, and a touch more sugar if the tomatoes were sharp.
  6. Meanwhile, make the grilled cheese: butter both outsides of the bread. Layer cheese between. In a frying pan over medium heat, cook 3 min per side until golden brown and oozy inside. Press with a spatula for crispness.
  7. Cut the sandwich diagonally. Ladle soup into a bowl. Scatter with a few torn basil leaves and a swirl of cream.
  8. Dunk immediately.
