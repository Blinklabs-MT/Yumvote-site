Thai Green Curry with Chicken

Coconut-creamy green curry packed with chicken, aubergine and Thai basil, ready in 25 minutes from a jarred paste. The Bangkok takeaway standby, made better at home.

25 minEasy~439 kcal / servingThai
Contains fish

Ingredients · per serving

  • 3 leaves Kaffir lime leaves
  • 1 medium Lime
  • 1 medium Red bell pepper
  • 200 g Chicken breast
  • 1 tbsp Fish sauce
  • 1 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • 1 small bunch Thai basil
  • 2 tbsp Thai green curry paste
  • 400 ml Coconut milk
  • to taste Salt
  • ½ medium Aubergine
  • 1 tsp Light brown sugar
  • 75 g Jasmine rice
  • 1 small Red chili
