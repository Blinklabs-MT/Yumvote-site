Spinach & Mushroom Frittata

Eggs baked with sautéed mushrooms, wilted spinach, ricotta dollops and parmesan — works hot, room temp or cold.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 3 tbsp Milk
  • small handful Fresh parsley
  • 200 g Mushrooms
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 30 g Parmesan
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 6 eggs Egg
  • to taste Salt
  • few sprigs Fresh thyme
  • 100 g Baby spinach
  • 80 g Ricotta

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 200°C.
  2. Heat olive oil in an oven-safe skillet. Sauté sliced mushrooms 6 min until golden. Add garlic + thyme, 30 sec.
  3. Add spinach in batches to wilt. Spread evenly.
  4. Whisk eggs + milk + salt + pepper. Pour over the veg.
  5. Dollop ricotta over the top in spoonfuls. Scatter parmesan.
  6. Bake 12-15 min until just set + golden at edges. Slide onto a board, cut wedges, top with parsley.
