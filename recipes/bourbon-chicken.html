Bourbon Chicken

American-Cajun-Chinese fusion (yes, really) — chicken cubes in a glossy bourbon-soy sauce with brown sugar, garlic and ginger. Mall food court icon, made better at home.

Ingredients · per serving

  • ¼ tsp Chili flakes
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 250 g Chicken thigh fillets
  • 3 tbsp Bourbon
  • 1 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • 1 tsp Fresh ginger
  • 3 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 100 g Broccoli florets
  • 1.5 tbsp Cornflour
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tbsp Apple cider vinegar
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds
  • 2 tbsp Tomato ketchup
  • 3 tbsp Light brown sugar
  • 75 g Jasmine rice

Instructions

  1. Rinse the rice in cold water. Combine with 150ml water, pinch salt. Boil, cover, lowest heat 12 min, rest 5 min off heat.
  2. Cube the chicken thighs into 2cm pieces. Pat dry. Toss with 1 tbsp cornflour.
  3. Mix the sauce: bourbon, soy sauce, brown sugar, apple cider vinegar, ketchup, garlic, ginger and chili flakes in a small bowl. Stir in 1 tsp cornflour mixed with 1 tbsp water.
  4. Heat the sunflower oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat. Add the chicken pieces in a single layer. Cook 5 minutes without stirring, then 3 minutes turning, until golden and cooked through.
  5. Pour the sauce over the chicken. Lower heat to medium. Simmer 4-5 minutes until the sauce thickens to a glossy glaze that coats the chicken.
  6. Off the heat, taste and adjust — more soy if not salty enough, more brown sugar if not sticky-sweet enough.
  7. Plate over the rice. Scatter with sliced spring onion and sesame seeds. Steamed broccoli alongside for green.
