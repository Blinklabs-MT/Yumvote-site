Kedgeree

Anglo-Indian rice dish — flaked smoked haddock with curried rice, peas and soft-boiled eggs. Originally Victorian breakfast, now an excellent dinner. 30 minutes.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 tbsp Curry powder
  • 1 leaf Bay leaf
  • 1 medium Lemon
  • 100 g Frozen peas
  • 3 tbsp Fresh parsley
  • 2 large Egg
  • 30 g Butter
  • 150 g Long-grain rice
  • 250 g Smoked haddock
  • to taste Salt
  • ½ tsp Ground turmeric
  • 300 ml Whole milk
  • 1 medium Onion

Instructions

  1. Poach the haddock: lay in a small pan, cover with the milk and a bay leaf. Bring to a gentle simmer, cover, lowest heat, 5-6 minutes until just opaque. Lift onto a plate. Strain and save the milk.
  2. Bring a small pot of water to the boil. Lower in the eggs gently. Boil 6 minutes for set whites with runny-soft yolks. Lift into cold water to stop cooking. Peel.
  3. Rinse the rice. Melt the butter in a wide pan over medium heat. Add the diced onion, cook 5 minutes until soft.
  4. Add the curry powder and ground turmeric. Stir 30 seconds to wake up the spices.
  5. Add the rice. Stir to coat. Pour in 300ml water (or the reserved poaching milk + water) and a pinch of salt. Bring to a simmer, cover, lower heat to lowest, cook 12 minutes.
  6. Off the heat. Add the frozen peas to the surface, cover, rest 5 minutes — the peas thaw and warm in the residual steam.
  7. Flake the haddock into chunks, discarding any skin and bones. Fold gently through the rice with the peas, chopped parsley and a squeeze of lemon.
  8. Plate the kedgeree. Halve the soft-boiled eggs and lay on top — burst the yolks at the table. Scatter more parsley, lemon wedge.
