Chicken Noodle Soup

Tender chicken, sweet carrots, celery, and noodles in a golden broth. The bowl that fixes a bad day.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 600 ml Chicken stock
  • 1 leaf Bay leaf
  • ¼ lemon Lemon
  • small handful Fresh parsley
  • 40 g Egg noodles (medium)
  • 100 g Carrot
  • 50 g Celery
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • ½ tsp Dried thyme
  • to taste Salt
  • 140 g Chicken thigh
  • 1 small onion Onion

Instructions

  1. Chop the onion, dice the carrots and celery into 5mm pieces. Mince the garlic.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion, carrot, and celery. Cook 6 min until soft.
  3. Add the garlic, dried thyme, and bay leaf. Stir 30 sec.
  4. Pour in the stock. Add the chicken thighs whole. Bring to a gentle simmer. Cover. Cook 15 min — chicken should be cooked through and shred easily.
  5. Lift the chicken onto a plate. Shred with two forks (it'll be very tender). Return to the pot.
  6. Add the noodles. Simmer 5 min — until just tender (check packet for time).
  7. Off the heat, stir in the chopped parsley and a squeeze of lemon. Taste — adjust salt and pepper.
  8. Lift out the bay leaf. Ladle into bowls.
