Turkey & Black Bean Power Bowl

Lean ground turkey seasoned Mexican-style with black beans, brown rice, corn, avocado, and lime. High protein, high fibre.

Ingredients · per serving

  • ½ tsp Dried oregano
  • ½ lime Lime
  • ½ avocado Avocado
  • 1 small tomato Tomato
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 140 g Turkey mince
  • pinch Chilli powder
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 60 g Sweetcorn
  • dashes Hot sauce
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • small handful Fresh coriander
  • 70 g Brown rice
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 120 g Tinned black beans
  • ½ onion Onion

Instructions

  1. Cook brown rice per packet (about 25 min or 2 min microwave pouch).
  2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat. Add chopped onion. Cook 4 min until soft. Add minced garlic, 30 sec.
  3. Add the turkey mince. Break up with a spoon. Cook 6 min until browned and no pink remains.
  4. Add smoked paprika, ground cumin, dried oregano, chilli powder (if using), salt, pepper. Stir 1 min.
  5. Add the drained black beans and 3 tbsp water. Simmer 3 min. Squeeze in lime juice. Taste — adjust salt.
  6. Quick guac: mash avocado with lime juice, pinch of salt, chopped coriander.
  7. Assemble the bowl: brown rice base. Turkey + beans on top. Spoonful of guac. Sweetcorn (drained from tin or thawed from frozen).
  8. Top with diced tomato, more coriander, hot sauce (optional). Lime wedge on the side.
