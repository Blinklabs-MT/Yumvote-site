Crispy Tofu & Veggie Stir-Fry

Golden cubes of tofu in a sticky soy-garlic glaze with crunchy stir-fried veg over rice.

30 minMedium~699 kcal / servingAsian
Contains soyContains sesameveganvegetariandairy free

Ingredients · per serving

  • 3 tbsp Neutral oil
  • 2 tbsp Light soy sauce
  • 120 g Cooked rice
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tsp Rice vinegar
  • ½ pepper Red pepper
  • 1 tsp grated Fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp Sweet chilli sauce
  • 2 tbsp Cornflour
  • 200 g Firm tofu
  • 2 onions Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds
  • 100 g Broccoli
