One-Pan Lemon Garlic Chicken & Rice

Chicken thighs cooked on top of rice that soaks up every drop of lemony, garlicky stock. One pan, no draining.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 180 ml Chicken stock
  • ½ lemon Lemon
  • small handful Fresh parsley
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 80 g Long-grain rice
  • ½ tsp Dried thyme
  • to taste Salt
  • 2 thighs Chicken thigh
  • ½ tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 small onion Onion

Instructions

  1. Pat the chicken thighs dry with kitchen paper. Season both sides with salt, pepper, and the paprika.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in an oven-safe pan with a lid (or a deep skillet) over medium-high heat.
  3. Place chicken skin-side down. Cook 5 min without moving — you want deep golden skin. Flip and cook 2 min more. Lift onto a plate.
  4. Preheat oven to 200°C (180°C fan).
  5. Lower heat to medium. Add the chopped onion to the same pan. Cook 4 min until soft. Add the minced garlic, cook 30 sec.
  6. Stir in the rice. Toast for 1 min — coats every grain in oil.
  7. Pour in the stock, lemon juice, lemon zest, and dried thyme. Stir once. Lay the chicken back on top, skin-side up. Don't stir after this — you want the rice to steam evenly.
  8. Bring to a simmer. Cover. Transfer to the oven. Bake 22 min.
  9. Lift the lid. Check rice is tender and stock absorbed. If wet, return uncovered 5 min.
  10. Rest 5 min off the heat with the lid back on. Scatter chopped parsley. Serve with lemon wedges.
