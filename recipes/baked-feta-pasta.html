Baked Feta Pasta

The viral TikTok recipe — a whole block of feta baked with cherry tomatoes and garlic until the cheese is creamy and the tomatoes burst into a sauce, tossed through penne with fresh basil. 30 minutes of impossible-not-to-love.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • to taste Black pepper
  • ½ tsp Chili flakes
  • 6 cloves Garlic
  • 250 g Penne
  • 6 tbsp Olive oil
  • 200 g Feta
  • 500 g Cherry tomatoes
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 small bunch Fresh basil

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C/200°C fan. Tip the cherry tomatoes into a baking dish.
  2. Place the whole block of feta in the centre of the tomatoes. Add the smashed garlic cloves around the feta.
  3. Drizzle with 4 tbsp olive oil, sprinkle with dried oregano, chili flakes, salt and pepper. Toss the tomatoes (not the feta) gently to coat.
  4. Bake 25-30 minutes until the tomatoes have burst and the feta is golden and soft.
  5. Meanwhile, bring a large pot of well-salted water to the boil. Cook the penne to packet time. Drain, reserving a mug of pasta water.
  6. Pull the baked tomato-feta dish from the oven. Mash with a fork or spoon — the feta should crush into the tomato juices, creating a thick creamy sauce.
  7. Tip the drained pasta into the dish with a splash of pasta water. Toss vigorously to coat every piece. Tear in fresh basil leaves. Plate, scatter more basil and a drizzle of olive oil. Black pepper.
