Smothered Chicken with Buttery Rice

Bone-in chicken thighs braised in a rich onion-mushroom-thyme gravy until fall-apart tender, served over buttery rice. Soul food slow Sunday classic.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 700 ml Chicken stock
  • 4 tbsp Plain flour
  • 3 tbsp Fresh parsley
  • 200 g Mushrooms
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • 60 g Butter
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 150 g Long-grain rice
  • 1 tsp Dried thyme
  • 200 g Tenderstem broccoli
  • to taste Salt
  • 4 sprigs Fresh thyme
  • 4 thighs Chicken thigh
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 2 medium Onion

Instructions

  1. Pat the chicken thighs dry. Season generously with salt, pepper, smoked paprika, garlic powder and dried thyme. Dust lightly with flour.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp sunflower oil in a wide heavy pan over medium-high heat. Lay the thighs in skin-side down. Cook 6-7 min until skin is deep gold and crispy. Flip, 3 min more. Lift onto a plate.
  3. Lower heat to medium. Melt the butter in the pan. Add thinly sliced onions and a pinch of salt. Cook 12-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until deep gold and jammy.
  4. Add the sliced mushrooms, cook 5 min until softened.
  5. Add minced garlic, fresh thyme and remaining flour. Stir 60 seconds.
  6. Slowly whisk in the chicken stock and Worcestershire sauce. Bring to a simmer, will thicken.
  7. Return the chicken (skin-side up) with any plate juices. Cover, lowest heat, simmer 25-30 min until fork-tender.
  8. Cook the rice: rinse, combine with 300ml chicken stock + 1 tbsp butter + pinch salt. Boil, cover, lowest heat 12 min, rest 5 min. Plate the buttery rice. Lay the chicken thigh on top, ladle gravy generously over. Steamed greens alongside, scatter chopped parsley.
