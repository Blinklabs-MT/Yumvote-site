Chicken Yakitori with Rice

Japanese izakaya skewers — chicken thigh chunks alternated with spring onion, basted in a sweet-savoury tare glaze, charred under the grill. 25 minutes, restaurant-grade.

Ingredients · per serving

  • ½ medium Cucumber
  • 3 tbsp Mirin
  • 250 g Chicken thigh fillets
  • 1 tsp Sunflower oil
  • 2 tbsp Sake
  • ½ tsp Caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp Fresh ginger
  • 4 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 4 stalks Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds
  • 2 tbsp Light brown sugar
  • 75 g Jasmine rice

Instructions

  1. Rinse the jasmine rice. Combine with 150ml water, pinch salt. Boil, cover, lowest heat 12 min, rest 5 min off heat.
  2. Cut the chicken thighs into 2cm cubes. Cut the white parts of the spring onions into 2cm chunks.
  3. Thread the chicken and spring onion alternately onto 4 skewers (3 chicken pieces, 2 spring onion, repeat).
  4. Make the tare: combine soy sauce, mirin, sake (or extra mirin), brown sugar and grated ginger in a small saucepan. Bring to a simmer over medium heat, reduce 4-5 minutes until glossy and syrupy.
  5. Heat the grill to high. Brush a baking tray with oil. Lay the skewers down.
  6. Grill 3 minutes, flip, brush with tare. Grill 2 minutes more, flip, brush again. Final 1-2 minutes per side, brushing once more — the glaze should be sticky and the chicken charred at the edges.
  7. Plate the rice in bowls. Lay 2 skewers on top each. Spoon any remaining tare over. Scatter with sliced green spring onion tops and sesame seeds. Quick pickled cucumber alongside (cucumber, rice vinegar, salt, sugar — toss 5 min).
