Turkish Köfte

Spice-laced lamb meatballs simmered in a garlicky tomato sauce. Comfort food across the Middle East and the Mediterranean.

Ingredients · per serving

  • small handful Fresh parsley
  • ½ tsp Sweet paprika
  • pinch Chilli flakes
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • ½ tsp Ground coriander
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 200 ml Tomato passata
  • ½ tsp Ground cumin
  • 120 g Lamb mince
  • ½ tsp Dried mint
  • to taste Salt
  • 70 g Onion

Instructions

  1. Make the meatballs: in a bowl, combine lamb mince, grated onion (squeeze out excess liquid in a clean tea towel first), 1 minced garlic clove, cumin, paprika, ground coriander, dried mint, salt, and pepper. Mix gently with your hands — overworking makes them tough.
  2. Form into walnut-sized balls (about 8 from 120g mince). Rest in the fridge 5 min while you start the sauce.
  3. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a wide frying pan over medium heat. Add finely chopped onion (for sauce), cook 5 min until soft.
  4. Add the second minced garlic clove and chilli flakes (if using). Stir 30 sec.
  5. Pour in the tomato passata + 50ml water. Simmer 5 min.
  6. Add the meatballs in a single layer. Don't stir for the first 2 min — let the bottoms set. Then gently roll them around to brown all sides, about 5 min total.
  7. Reduce heat to low, partially cover. Simmer 10 min — meatballs cook through and the sauce thickens.
  8. Taste, adjust seasoning. Scatter parsley. Serve with rice, flatbread, or pasta.
