Thai Basil Chicken (Pad Krapow)

Bangkok street-food staple. Minced chicken stir-fried with chillies, garlic, and holy basil. Served over rice with a crispy fried egg.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 120 g Chicken mince
  • small handful Thai holy basil
  • 1 tsp Fish sauce
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Oyster sauce
  • 2 tbsp Vegetable oil
  • 1 large Egg
  • 3 whole Bird's eye chillies
  • 1 tbsp Water
  • 1 tsp Soy sauce
  • 75 g Jasmine rice
  • 1 tsp Sugar

Instructions

  1. Put the chicken mince in a bowl, break it up with a fork.
  2. On a chopping board, smash the garlic cloves flat with the side of a knife, peel, and finely chop. Do the same with the chillies (use gloves or wash hands well afterwards — don't touch your eyes).
  3. Mix the sauce in a small bowl: oyster sauce, soy sauce, fish sauce, sugar, and water.
  4. Heat 1 tablespoon of the oil in a wok or large pan over high heat.
  5. Add the garlic and chillies. Stir for 15 seconds — they'll sizzle and smell sharp.
  6. Add the chicken mince. Break it up with the spatula and stir-fry for 3 minutes until no pink remains and any liquid has cooked off.
  7. Pour in the sauce. Toss for 1 minute until everything is coated and glossy.
  8. Tear the basil leaves in roughly and toss for 10 seconds — they should just wilt. Off the heat.
  9. In a separate pan, fry the egg in 1 tablespoon of hot oil until the edges are crispy and brown but the yolk is still runny, about 2 minutes.
  10. Plate the rice. Spoon the chicken alongside. Top with the fried egg. Serve with extra chillies for whoever wants more heat.
