Thai Basil Chicken (Pad Krapow)

Bangkok street-food staple. Minced chicken stir-fried with chillies, garlic, and holy basil. Served over rice with a crispy fried egg.

20 minMedium~522 kcal / servingAsian
Contains glutenContains eggsContains soyContains fishdairy free

Ingredients · per serving

  • 120 g Chicken mince
  • small handful Thai holy basil
  • 1 tsp Fish sauce
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Oyster sauce
  • 2 tbsp Vegetable oil
  • 1 large Egg
  • 3 whole Bird's eye chillies
  • 1 tbsp Water
  • 1 tsp Soy sauce
  • 75 g Jasmine rice
  • 1 tsp Sugar
