Steak Pita with Tzatziki

Warm pita stuffed with sliced rare steak, crisp lettuce, tomato, red onion, and cooling cucumber-yogurt tzatziki.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 pita Pita bread
  • 60 g Cucumber
  • ½ lemon Lemon
  • 40 g Cos lettuce
  • small handful Fresh parsley
  • 1 tomato Tomato
  • to taste Black pepper
  • pinch Chilli flakes
  • 1 clove Garlic
  • small handful Fresh dill
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Vegetable oil
  • 4 tbsp Greek yogurt
  • 20 g Red onion
  • 150 g Steak (sirloin)
  • to taste Salt

Instructions

  1. Tzatziki: grate cucumber + squeeze dry. Mix with Greek yogurt + minced garlic + dill + lemon + salt + pepper + drop of olive oil.
  2. Pat steak dry. Season generously with salt + pepper. Heat heavy pan smoking-hot with 1 tsp oil. Sear 2-3 min per side for medium-rare. Rest 5 min, slice thin.
  3. Warm pita briefly in dry pan or microwave wrapped in damp paper.
  4. Open pita pocket. Spread tzatziki inside.
  5. Stuff with shredded lettuce + sliced tomato + red onion + steak slices.
  6. Top with extra tzatziki, parsley, chili flakes, drizzle olive oil.
