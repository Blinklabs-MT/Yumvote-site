Garlic Mushroom & Lentil Bowl

Slow-cooked garlic mushrooms over creamy lentils with kale, topped with a poached egg and crispy sourdough. British-comfort vegetarian bowl, 30 minutes.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 100 g Puy lentils
  • 1 leaf Bay leaf
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley
  • 250 g Mushrooms
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 20 g Parmesan
  • 80 g Kale
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 large Egg
  • 1 slice Sourdough bread
  • 1 tsp Soy sauce
  • 20 g Butter
  • 1 tbsp White wine vinegar
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Fresh thyme
  • 50 ml Double cream

Instructions

  1. Rinse the lentils. Combine with 600ml water, a bay leaf and a crushed garlic clove in a pot. Bring to a boil, lower to simmer, cook 20-25 minutes until tender. Drain. Stir in the cream, salt and pepper.
  2. Slice the mushrooms thickly. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a wide pan over high heat. Add the mushrooms in a single layer. Don't move for 2 minutes — let them get gold and brown.
  3. Stir, add the butter, minced garlic, thyme. Cook 5-6 minutes more until deeply browned. Splash in the soy sauce (yes — adds umami without changing the flavour), toss 30 seconds.
  4. Wilt the kale: strip from stems, tear into pieces. Add to the mushroom pan with a splash of water. Wilt 60 seconds. Season with salt.
  5. Poach the egg: bring a pot of water to a gentle simmer. Add 1 tbsp white wine vinegar. Crack the egg into a cup, stir the water to a swirl, drop the egg in. Cook 3 minutes for runny yolk. Lift out with a slotted spoon.
  6. Toast the sourdough: brush with olive oil, toast in a hot dry pan until golden each side, rub with a cut garlic clove.
  7. Build the bowls: creamy lentils as base. Pile on the garlic mushrooms and wilted kale. Top with the poached egg in the centre — burst the yolk to coat. Scatter parsley and grated parmesan. Crispy sourdough on the side.
