Sticky Sesame Cauliflower

Cornflour-crusted cauliflower florets oven-roasted to crisp, tossed in a sticky sesame-honey-soy glaze, served over jasmine rice with edamame. Better-than-takeaway vegetarian, 30 minutes.

Ingredients · per serving

  • to taste Black pepper
  • ¼ tsp Chili flakes
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp Fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 3 tbsp Honey
  • 350 g Cauliflower
  • 3 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 3 tbsp Cornflour
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 100 g Edamame beans
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds
  • 75 g Jasmine rice

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C/200°C fan. Rinse the rice and put on to cook (150ml water, lowest heat 12 min covered, rest 5 min).
  2. Cut the cauliflower into bite-size florets. Toss in a big bowl with 1 tbsp sunflower oil, cornflour, salt and pepper until each piece is dusted.
  3. Spread on a baking tray lined with parchment in a single layer. Don't crowd — they need to crisp, not steam.
  4. Roast 18-22 minutes, turning once, until deep gold and crispy at the edges.
  5. Make the glaze in a small saucepan: soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, sesame oil, minced garlic, grated ginger, water and the cornflour slurry. Bubble 2 minutes over medium heat until glossy and thick.
  6. Tip the roasted cauliflower into the saucepan. Toss to coat evenly in the glaze.
  7. Steam the edamame 3 minutes in salted water, drain. Plate the rice. Pile cauliflower on top. Scatter edamame, sesame seeds, sliced spring onion and chili flakes if using.
