Penne all'Arrabbiata

"Angry" pasta — sharp with garlic, hot with chilli, bright with tomato. Five ingredients, fifteen minutes, full-on flavour.

15 minEasy~390 kcal / servingItalian

Ingredients · per serving

  • small handful Fresh parsley
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 1 tsp Chilli flakes
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 100 g Penne
  • 300 g Tinned whole peeled tomatoes
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • to taste Salt

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the penne. Cook to packet time minus 1 min.
  2. Meanwhile, slice the garlic THIN (paper-thin slices = more flavour and gentler than minced). Roughly chop the parsley.
  3. Heat the olive oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Add the sliced garlic and the chilli flakes (or whole chillies if using).
  4. Cook 1-2 min, swirling the pan — garlic should turn light gold, NOT brown (brown garlic = bitter). The oil takes on a chilli colour.
  5. Add the tinned tomatoes (crush them with your hands as you add — or pulse first). Season with a generous pinch of salt and pepper.
  6. Simmer 6 min, breaking up any large tomato chunks with a wooden spoon. Sauce should look thick and red.
  7. Just before pasta is done, scoop 100ml pasta water with a mug. Drain pasta.
  8. Tip the drained pasta straight into the sauce pan. Add a splash of pasta water. Toss vigorously 1 min — pasta absorbs the sauce.
  9. Off the heat, scatter most of the chopped parsley. Toss again.
  10. Plate. Top with the remaining parsley, a final drizzle of olive oil, and a grind of black pepper. Some Romans add pecorino — purists don't. Your call.
