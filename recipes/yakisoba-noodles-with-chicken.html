Yakisoba Noodles with Chicken

Japanese street-stall noodles — wheat noodles stir-fried hard with chicken, cabbage and a sweet-savoury yakisoba sauce, topped with bonito flakes and pickled ginger. 25 minutes, restaurant standard.

25 minMedium~673 kcal / servingJapanese
Contains glutenContains soyContains fishContains shellfish

Ingredients · per serving

  • 150 g White cabbage
  • 1 medium Carrot
  • 180 g Chicken thigh fillets
  • 1 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • 1 tbsp Oyster sauce
  • ½ tsp Caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp Bonito flakes
  • 200 g Yakisoba noodles
  • 2 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tbsp Pickled ginger
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • 1 tbsp Tomato ketchup
  • ½ medium Onion
