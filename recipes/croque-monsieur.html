Croque Monsieur

French grilled ham and cheese — elevated. Béchamel-soaked bread with ham and Gruyère, grilled until golden and bubbling.

25 minMedium~559 kcal / servingFrench

Ingredients · per serving

  • 15 g Plain flour
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 slices Bread
  • 80 g Gruyère cheese
  • pinch Nutmeg
  • 15 g Butter
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • to taste Salt
  • 60 g Cooked ham
  • 120 ml Whole milk

Instructions

  1. Make the béchamel: melt the butter in a small saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in the flour, cook 1 min.
  2. SLOWLY pour in the warm milk while whisking constantly to keep it smooth. Simmer 3 min until thickened to a creamy sauce.
  3. Off the heat, stir in 30g of the grated cheese, the Dijon mustard, a pinch of salt and pepper, and a tiny grating of nutmeg.
  4. Heat your oven grill (broiler) to high.
  5. Lightly toast the bread slices: 1 min per side under the grill until pale gold but still soft.
  6. Spread a thin layer of béchamel on each toasted slice.
  7. Lay the ham slices on one slice of bread. Top with half the remaining grated cheese. Close the sandwich.
  8. Slather more béchamel generously on top of the closed sandwich. Sprinkle the rest of the cheese all over the top.
  9. Place on a baking tray. Slide under the grill 3-4 min until the cheese is golden, bubbling, and slightly charred at the edges.
  10. Serve immediately — best eaten while the cheese is still molten.
