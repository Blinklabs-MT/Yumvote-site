Breakfast Burrito Bowl

Scrambled eggs over hash browns with black beans, salsa, avocado, cheese and chipotle sour cream — burrito-as-bowl.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 lime Lime
  • ½ avocado Avocado
  • 1 tomato Tomato
  • 120 g Frozen hash browns
  • to taste Black pepper
  • ½ tsp Chipotle in adobo
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 3 eggs Egg
  • 2 tbsp Sour cream
  • 40 g Mature cheddar
  • ½ tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp Butter
  • ½ chilli Jalapeño
  • small handful Fresh coriander
  • 30 g Red onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 120 g Tinned black beans
  • ½ tsp Garlic powder

Instructions

  1. Crisp hash browns in olive oil 5 min per side until golden.
  2. Warm black beans with cumin + garlic powder + splash of water.
  3. Make pico: dice tomato + red onion + jalapeño + coriander, toss with lime + salt.
  4. Scramble eggs in butter slow + low until soft + custardy.
  5. Bowl: hash browns → beans → eggs → pico → avocado → grated cheese.
  6. Drizzle chipotle sour cream. Top with coriander + hot sauce.
