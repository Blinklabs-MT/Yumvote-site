Kale Caesar Salad with Salmon

Crispy-skin salmon over a massaged kale caesar with parmesan, croutons and capers. The classic Caesar reimagined with hearty greens that stand up to the dressing.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 250 g Salmon fillet (skinless)
  • 1 medium Lemon
  • 4 tbsp Mayonnaise
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 50 g Parmesan
  • 200 g Kale
  • 4 tbsp Olive oil
  • 80 g Sourdough bread
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tbsp Capers
  • ½ tsp Garlic powder
  • 3 fillets Anchovy fillets

Instructions

  1. Pat the salmon fillets dry. Season the skin generously with salt and pepper.
  2. Make the croutons: cube the bread, toss with 1 tbsp olive oil, garlic powder, salt and pepper. Toast in a hot dry pan 5 min until deeply golden.
  3. Strip the kale from its tough stems, tear into bite-size pieces. Drizzle with 1 tsp olive oil and a pinch of salt. Massage with your hands for 2 minutes until darkened and silky.
  4. Make the dressing: whisk mayonnaise, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, minced anchovy, minced garlic, Worcestershire, grated parmesan, salt and pepper. Loosen with 1-2 tbsp water if too thick.
  5. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a non-stick pan over high heat. Lay salmon skin-side down. Cook 4 min, then flip, 2 min more. Lift onto a plate.
  6. Toss the massaged kale with most of the dressing. Plate in shallow bowls.
  7. Top with the salmon (skin-side up), croutons, shaved parmesan and a scattering of capers. Drizzle remaining dressing over the salmon. Lemon wedge.
