Chickpea & Spinach Stew

Andalusian tapas classic scaled up to a main. Smoky paprika, cumin, garlic, and a generous handful of greens. Plant-based comfort.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 100 ml Vegetable stock
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tsp Sherry vinegar
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 80 ml Tomato passata
  • 1 tsp Sweet smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 200 g Chickpeas (tinned)
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 slice Crusty bread
  • 100 g Spinach
  • 50 g Onion

Instructions

  1. Drain and rinse the chickpeas. Roughly chop the onion and garlic.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Add the onion, cook 5 min until soft.
  3. Add the garlic, smoked paprika, and cumin. Stir 30 seconds — careful with the paprika, it burns easily.
  4. Add the chickpeas and tomato passata. Stir to coat in the spice.
  5. Pour in the stock. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  6. Cook uncovered 8 min — mash about a quarter of the chickpeas with the back of a spoon as you stir. This thickens the stew naturally.
  7. Add the spinach in big handfuls. Stir until wilted, 1-2 min.
  8. Stir in the sherry vinegar. Taste, season with salt and pepper. Serve in a deep bowl with crusty bread for dipping.
