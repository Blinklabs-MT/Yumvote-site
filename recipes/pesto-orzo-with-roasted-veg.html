Pesto Orzo with Roasted Veg

Buttery orzo coated in basil pesto, scattered with sheet-pan roasted courgette, peppers and cherry tomato.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 100 g Orzo
  • ¼ lemon Lemon
  • 1 courgette Courgette
  • 3 tbsp Basil pesto
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 20 g Parmesan
  • 60 g Mozzarella
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 pepper Red pepper
  • 1 tbsp Butter
  • 120 g Cherry tomatoes
  • to taste Salt
  • small handful Fresh basil

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven 220°C. Cube courgette + pepper. Halve cherry tomatoes. Toss with oil + salt + pepper on tray.
  2. Roast 18 min until edges caramelize.
  3. Cook orzo in salted water. Reserve mug pasta water.
  4. In wide bowl, toss hot orzo with pesto + splash pasta water + butter until glossy.
  5. Fold in roasted veg + juices.
  6. Top with torn mozzarella, basil + parmesan + olive oil + lemon zest.
