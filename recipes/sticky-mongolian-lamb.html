Sticky Mongolian Lamb

Lamb strips stir-fried in a glossy soy-hoisin-ginger glaze with spring onions and chili. The takeaway-classic given a richer, deeper twist with lamb instead of beef.

25 minEasy~573 kcal / servingChinese
Contains soyContains glutenContains sesame

Ingredients · per serving

  • ½ medium Lime
  • 1 medium Red bell pepper
  • 300 g Lamb leg steak
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 1.5 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 2 tsp Fresh ginger
  • 4 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 tbsp Cornflour
  • 4 stalks Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 2 tbsp Hoisin sauce
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds
  • 2 tbsp Light brown sugar
  • 75 g Jasmine rice
  • ½ medium Onion
  • 1 small Red chili
