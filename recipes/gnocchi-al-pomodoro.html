Gnocchi al Pomodoro

Pillowy potato dumplings tossed in a quick fresh tomato sauce with garlic and basil. From-scratch gnocchi is a project; shop-bought is here in 25 minutes.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 200 g Potato gnocchi
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 20 g Parmesan
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • 150 g Cherry tomatoes
  • to taste Salt
  • small handful Fresh basil
  • 150 g Tinned chopped tomatoes
  • pinch Sugar

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil for the gnocchi.
  2. Meanwhile, make the sauce: thinly slice the garlic. Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a large frying pan over medium heat. Add garlic. Cook 1-2 min — should turn light gold (not brown).
  3. Add the cherry tomatoes (halved). Cook 4 min, stirring, until they release juice and start to collapse into a saucy mess. Press some with a wooden spoon to encourage them.
  4. Add the tinned tomatoes (hand-crush as you tip them in, or pulse first). Add a pinch of salt, pepper, sugar.
  5. Simmer 6 min — sauce should thicken and look glossy.
  6. Drop the gnocchi into the boiling water. Cook until they FLOAT to the surface — usually 2-3 min for shop-bought. Don't overcook (gluey).
  7. Lift the gnocchi out with a slotted spoon straight into the sauce (don't drain — a little pasta water adds to the silk).
  8. Tear in most of the basil. Toss gently — gnocchi are fragile, fold rather than stir.
  9. Off the heat, drizzle with olive oil. Plate. Top with grated parmesan, remaining basil leaves, a grind of black pepper.
