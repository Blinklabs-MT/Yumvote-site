Roasted Butternut Squash Soup

Squash roasted until caramelised, blended with stock, garlic, and a hint of ginger. Velvety, autumnal, naturally sweet.

50 minEasy~301 kcal / servingMediterranean
Contains dairyvegetariangluten free

Ingredients · per serving

  • 400 ml Vegetable stock
  • ¼ lemon Lemon
  • small handful Fresh parsley
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tbsp Greek yoghurt
  • 400 g Butternut squash
  • 1 tsp grated Fresh ginger
  • ½ tsp Ground cumin
  • 40 ml Double cream or coconut cream
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 onion Onion
  • 1 tbsp Pumpkin or sunflower seeds
