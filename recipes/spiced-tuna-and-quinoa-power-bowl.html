Spiced Tuna & Quinoa Power Bowl

Seared tuna steak with a spice-rubbed crust over quinoa with roasted sweet potato, kale and a sweet-spicy mango salsa. Modern protein-bowl with bright tropical flavours, 30 minutes.

30 minMedium~554 kcal / servingAmerican
Contains fishContains sesame

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 medium Lime
  • 200 g Sweet potato
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 1 tsp Sunflower oil
  • 200 g Sushi-grade tuna steak
  • 80 g Kale
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • ½ medium Ripe mango
  • 75 g Quinoa
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 small bunch Fresh coriander
  • ¼ medium Red onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds
  • 1 tsp Light brown sugar
  • 2 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • ½ small Red chili
