Spiced Tuna & Quinoa Power Bowl

Seared tuna steak with a spice-rubbed crust over quinoa with roasted sweet potato, kale and a sweet-spicy mango salsa. Modern protein-bowl with bright tropical flavours, 30 minutes.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 medium Lime
  • 200 g Sweet potato
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 1 tsp Sunflower oil
  • 200 g Sushi-grade tuna steak
  • 80 g Kale
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • ½ medium Ripe mango
  • 75 g Quinoa
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 small bunch Fresh coriander
  • ¼ medium Red onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds
  • 1 tsp Light brown sugar
  • 2 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • ½ small Red chili

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C/200°C fan. Cube the sweet potato into 2cm pieces. Toss with 1 tbsp olive oil, smoked paprika, salt and pepper on a baking tray. Roast 22 minutes until tender with caramelised edges.
  2. Rinse the quinoa. Combine with 150ml water, pinch salt. Boil, cover, lowest heat 12 min, rest 5 min off heat.
  3. Massage the kale: strip from stems, tear into bite-size pieces. Drizzle with 1 tsp olive oil and pinch of salt. Massage 2 min until silky.
  4. Make the mango salsa: dice the mango small. Mix with chopped red chili, red onion, coriander, lime juice and a pinch of salt.
  5. Make the spice rub: combine smoked paprika, ground cumin, brown sugar, garlic powder, salt and black pepper. Pat the tuna steak completely dry. Press the rub onto all surfaces.
  6. Heat a heavy frying pan over very high heat until smoking. Pour in 1 tsp sunflower oil. Lay the tuna in. Sear 60 seconds per long side (4 sides — rectangle), 4 minutes total — centre stays vibrant ruby.
  7. Lift tuna onto a board. Rest 2 minutes. Slice across the grain into 5mm slices.
  8. Build the bowls: quinoa as base, top with sweet potato, massaged kale, mango salsa and fanned tuna slices. Drizzle with extra olive oil and lime. Scatter sesame seeds.
