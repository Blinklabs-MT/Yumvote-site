Egg & Spinach Protein Wrap

Wholemeal wrap filled with scrambled eggs, sautéed spinach, feta, and avocado. 30g+ protein, lunch-or-dinner friendly.

Ingredients · per serving

  • ½ avocado Avocado
  • to taste Black pepper
  • pinch Chilli flakes
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 3 eggs Egg
  • 30 g Feta
  • 50 g Cherry tomatoes
  • to taste Salt
  • 60 g Baby spinach
  • 1 wrap Wholemeal tortilla wrap

Instructions

  1. Beat the eggs in a bowl with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat 0.5 tbsp olive oil in a small non-stick frying pan over medium heat. Add the spinach. Cook 1 min until wilted. Tip onto a plate, press out water.
  3. Add another 0.5 tbsp oil to the same pan. Lower heat to low. Pour in the eggs. Stir slowly with a spatula 1.5 min — soft, just set. Don't overcook (rubber eggs are sad eggs).
  4. Warm the wrap: in a dry pan over medium heat, 20 sec per side. Or microwave 10 sec wrapped in a paper towel.
  5. Lay the wrap flat. Spread mashed avocado in a line down the middle.
  6. Top with the scrambled eggs, the wilted spinach, and crumbled feta. Add a few cherry tomato halves if using.
  7. A pinch of chilli flakes or hot sauce if you're feeling spicy.
  8. Roll: fold in the sides, then roll from the bottom up tight. Cut on the diagonal.
