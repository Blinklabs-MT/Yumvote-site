Smothered Cabbage with Bacon

Wedges of cabbage slow-braised with smoky bacon, onion, garlic and broth until silky-tender, finished with cider vinegar. Soul-food vegetable side that earns main-course status with crusty cornbread.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 400 ml Chicken stock
  • 1 medium head Green cabbage
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley
  • to taste Black pepper
  • ¼ tsp Chili flakes
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 2 tbsp Cider vinegar
  • to taste Hot sauce
  • 120 g Smoked bacon
  • 4 slices Sourdough bread
  • 20 g Butter
  • 1 tsp Dried thyme
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 medium Onion

Instructions

  1. Cut the green cabbage into 4-6 wedges through the core (so they hold together).
  2. Dice the bacon. Heat a wide heavy lidded pot over medium heat. Crisp the bacon 5-6 min until golden. Lift onto kitchen paper.
  3. Lower heat to medium. Add the sliced onion to the bacon fat. Cook 6 min until softened.
  4. Add minced garlic, smoked paprika, chili flakes, dried thyme, salt and pepper. Stir 30 seconds.
  5. Pour in the chicken stock and cider vinegar. Bring to a gentle simmer.
  6. Lay the cabbage wedges into the broth (cut-side down). Sprinkle the crispy bacon over.
  7. Cover, lowest heat, simmer 20-25 minutes until the cabbage is fork-tender at the core (test with a knife).
  8. Crank heat up briefly to reduce broth a touch. Off the heat, swirl in a knob of butter. Plate 1-2 cabbage wedges per portion, spoon broth and bacon generously over. Scatter chopped parsley. Crusty cornbread or buttered toast alongside for dunking. A drizzle of hot sauce optional.
