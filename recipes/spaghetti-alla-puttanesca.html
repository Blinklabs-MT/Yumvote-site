Spaghetti alla Puttanesca

The 25-minute Roman pantry pasta — olives, capers, anchovies, garlic and tomato, salty and punchy and ready before the kettle's cooled. The Italian answer to 'we have nothing in.'

Ingredients · per serving

  • to taste Fresh parsley
  • ½ tsp Chili flakes
  • 100 g Spaghetti
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 50 g Kalamata olives
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • to taste Salt
  • 200 g Tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tbsp Capers
  • 3 fillets Anchovy fillets

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a rolling boil. Drop in the spaghetti and cook to packet time.
  2. While the pasta cooks, finely chop the garlic. Slice the olives. Drain the capers. Roughly chop the anchovy fillets.
  3. Heat the olive oil in a wide pan over medium-low heat. Add the garlic, anchovies and chili flakes. Cook 1-2 minutes — the garlic should sizzle gently and the anchovies dissolve into the oil. Don't let the garlic colour.
  4. Tip in the olives and capers. Stir for 30 seconds.
  5. Pour in the tinned tomatoes. Lower heat, simmer 8-10 minutes until thickened. Crush any whole tomatoes with the back of a spoon. Taste — it shouldn't need salt (capers + anchovies + olives).
  6. Lift the spaghetti straight into the sauce with tongs (don't drain — bring some pasta water with you). Toss vigorously over high heat for 1 minute. The starchy water emulsifies into the sauce and makes it cling.
  7. Plate, scatter chopped parsley, finish with a drizzle of olive oil. Skip the cheese — Italians don't, and it muddies the punchy salt.
