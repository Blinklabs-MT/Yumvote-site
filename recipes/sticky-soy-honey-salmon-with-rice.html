Sticky Soy-Honey Salmon with Rice

Salmon fillets glazed with a sticky soy-honey-ginger reduction, blistered under the grill until the surface caramelises, served over jasmine rice with broccoli. 20 minutes, restaurant-quality.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 250 g Salmon fillet (skinless)
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tsp Sunflower oil
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp Fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 3 tbsp Honey
  • 3 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 150 g Broccoli florets
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds
  • 75 g Jasmine rice

Instructions

  1. Rinse the rice. Combine with 150ml water and pinch salt. Boil, cover, lowest heat 12 min, rest 5 min off heat.
  2. Heat the grill to high. Line a baking tray with foil and brush lightly with oil.
  3. Mix the soy sauce, honey, rice vinegar, grated ginger, minced garlic and sesame oil in a small bowl.
  4. Pat the salmon fillets dry. Lay skin-side down on the tray. Brush generously with half the glaze.
  5. Grill for 4 minutes. Brush with more glaze. Grill another 4 minutes — the surface should be deeply caramelised and sticky, the centre just opaque. Watch close — honey burns fast.
  6. Meanwhile, steam the broccoli florets in a steamer or microwave 4 minutes until just tender.
  7. Plate the rice in bowls. Lay the salmon on top, broccoli alongside. Spoon any remaining glaze from the tray over the salmon. Scatter sesame seeds and sliced spring onion.
