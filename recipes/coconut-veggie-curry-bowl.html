Coconut Veggie Curry Bowl

Sweet potato, cauliflower and chickpeas in a creamy coconut curry sauce with spinach, over jasmine rice. Vegan curry bowl that satisfies hard, ready in 30 minutes.

30 minEasy~585 kcal / servingIndian
Contains gluten

Ingredients · per serving

  • 150 ml Vegetable stock
  • 1.5 tbsp Curry powder
  • ½ medium Lemon
  • 250 g Sweet potato
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 2 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • 1 tsp Ground coriander
  • 400 ml Coconut milk
  • 2 tsp Fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 250 g Cauliflower
  • 1 tbsp Tomato purée
  • 1 small bunch Fresh coriander
  • 1 small Naan
  • 240 g Tinned chickpeas
  • to taste Salt
  • 80 g Baby spinach
  • 75 g Jasmine rice
  • ½ tsp Ground turmeric
  • 1 medium Onion
