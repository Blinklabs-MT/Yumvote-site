Pesto Chicken & Halloumi Tray

Pesto-marinated chicken thighs and halloumi roasted with cherry tomatoes, courgette and red onion. A one-tray Mediterranean dinner with no washing up.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 180 ml Vegetable stock
  • 1 medium Lemon
  • 1 medium Courgette
  • 5 tbsp Basil pesto
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 300 g Chicken thigh fillets
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 200 g Halloumi
  • 100 g Couscous
  • 200 g Cherry tomatoes
  • 1 medium Red onion
  • to taste Salt
  • to taste Fresh basil

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C/200°C fan. Cube the courgette and red onion into 2cm chunks. Halve the cherry tomatoes (or leave whole if small).
  2. Marinate the chicken: cube the thighs into 3cm pieces. Toss with 3 tbsp pesto, salt and pepper. Let sit 10 min.
  3. Tip the veg onto a large baking tray. Drizzle with 1 tbsp olive oil, oregano, salt and pepper. Toss.
  4. Roast the veg 12 minutes — gives them a head start.
  5. Add the marinated chicken to the tray, spread amongst the veg.
  6. Roast 15 minutes.
  7. Slice the halloumi into 1cm slabs. Pat dry. Add to the tray for the final 10-12 minutes — it should char and crisp at the edges.
  8. Take the tray out. Squeeze lemon over everything. Scatter fresh basil leaves and a final spoonful of pesto dotted around. Plate over couscous or with crusty bread.
