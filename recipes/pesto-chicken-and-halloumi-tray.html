Pesto Chicken & Halloumi Tray

Pesto-marinated chicken thighs and halloumi roasted with cherry tomatoes, courgette and red onion. A one-tray Mediterranean dinner with no washing up.

35 minEasy~394 kcal / servingMediterranean
Contains dairyContains nutsContains gluten

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 180 ml Vegetable stock
  • 1 medium Lemon
  • 1 medium Courgette
  • 5 tbsp Basil pesto
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 300 g Chicken thigh fillets
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 200 g Halloumi
  • 100 g Couscous
  • 200 g Cherry tomatoes
  • 1 medium Red onion
  • to taste Salt
  • to taste Fresh basil
