Tuna Crunch Wrap

Tuna mayo with sweetcorn + celery + spring onion, rolled with lettuce + tomato + a kettle-chip crunch layer.

10 minEasy~356 kcal / servingBright Plates
Contains glutenContains meatContains eggsContains mustardpescatarian

Ingredients · per serving

  • 20 g Kettle crisps
  • ½ lemon Lemon
  • 2 tbsp Mayonnaise
  • 30 g Baby lettuce
  • ½ tomato Tomato
  • 20 g Celery
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 1 tortilla Large flour tortilla
  • 40 g Sweetcorn
  • 120 g Tuna in olive oil
  • ½ tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
