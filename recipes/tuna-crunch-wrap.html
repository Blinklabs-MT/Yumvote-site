Tuna Crunch Wrap

Tuna mayo with sweetcorn + celery + spring onion, rolled with lettuce + tomato + a kettle-chip crunch layer.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 20 g Kettle crisps
  • ½ lemon Lemon
  • 2 tbsp Mayonnaise
  • 30 g Baby lettuce
  • ½ tomato Tomato
  • 20 g Celery
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 1 tortilla Large flour tortilla
  • 40 g Sweetcorn
  • 120 g Tuna in olive oil
  • ½ tsp Dijon mustard
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • to taste Salt

Instructions

  1. Drain tuna. Mix with mayo + finely diced celery + sweetcorn + spring onion + lemon juice + dijon + salt + pepper.
  2. Warm tortilla in dry pan 20 sec per side.
  3. Spread tuna mix in middle. Layer baby lettuce + sliced tomato.
  4. Crumble a handful of kettle chips over the top — that's the crunch.
  5. Fold sides in, roll tight. Cut diagonally.
  6. Eat fast — crisps soften within 10 min.
