Crispy Tofu with Honey-Sesame Glaze

Cornflour-crusted tofu pan-fried golden, tossed in a sticky honey-sesame-soy glaze, served over jasmine rice with steamed broccoli. Faster than ordering takeaway, and way better for you.

25 minEasy~705 kcal / servingChinese
Contains soyContains sesame

Ingredients · per serving

  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 2 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp Fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 3 tbsp Honey
  • 2 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 100 g Broccoli florets
  • 3 tbsp Cornflour
  • 250 g Firm tofu
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Sesame seeds
  • 75 g Jasmine rice
  • ½ tsp Smoked paprika
