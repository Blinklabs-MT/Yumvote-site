Sushi Bowl with Crispy Salmon

Crispy-skin salmon flaked over sushi rice with avocado, cucumber, edamame, nori strips and a soy-sesame glaze.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 60 g Cucumber
  • 150 g Salmon fillet (skinless)
  • ½ sheet Nori sheet
  • 1 tsp Toasted sesame seeds
  • ½ avocado Avocado
  • 1 tsp Vegetable oil
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 50 g Edamame (shelled)
  • ½ tsp Fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 1 tsp Honey
  • 1.5 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 100 g Sushi rice
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • pinch Sugar

Instructions

  1. Pat salmon skin BONE-DRY. Salt generously. Heat oil in a non-stick pan over medium-high. Lay salmon skin-down, press flat with a spatula 30 sec.
  2. Cook 4 min skin-down without moving. Flip, cook 2 min. Skin should be crackly.
  3. Whisk soy + honey + rice vinegar + sesame oil + grated ginger for the glaze.
  4. Sticky the rice: warm cooked rice + 1 tsp rice vinegar + pinch salt + pinch sugar.
  5. Flake salmon (keep skin attached for crunch). Bowl: rice → salmon → avocado → cucumber → edamame.
  6. Drizzle glaze. Top with nori strips, sesame seeds, scallion. Optional: spicy mayo for the camera.
