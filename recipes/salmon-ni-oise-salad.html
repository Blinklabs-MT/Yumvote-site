Salmon Niçoise Salad

Pan-seared salmon over a classic Provençal salad of new potatoes, green beans, hard-boiled egg, olives, cherry tomatoes and anchovy-Dijon vinaigrette. The summer lunch with serious dinner credentials.

30 minEasy~423 kcal / servingFrench
Contains fishContains eggs

Ingredients · per serving

  • 250 g Salmon fillet (skinless)
  • 1 medium Lemon
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 1 clove Garlic
  • 50 g Kalamata olives
  • 5 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 large Egg
  • 2 tbsp Red wine vinegar
  • 150 g Green beans
  • 150 g Cherry tomatoes
  • 80 g Mixed salad leaves
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • to taste Salt
  • 250 g New potatoes
  • 2 fillets Anchovy fillets
