Pasta e Fagioli

Hearty Italian stew of small pasta and white beans in a rich tomato-rosemary broth. Peasant food that feeds armies.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 500 ml Vegetable stock
  • 1 sprig Fresh rosemary
  • 60 g Carrot
  • 40 g Celery
  • to taste Black pepper
  • pinch Chilli flakes
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 15 g Parmesan
  • 60 g Small pasta
  • 2.5 tbsp Olive oil
  • 240 g Tinned cannellini or borlotti beans
  • to taste Salt
  • piece Parmesan rind
  • 200 g Tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 1 small onion Onion

Instructions

  1. Heat 2 tbsp olive oil in a pot over medium heat. Add the chopped onion, diced carrot, and celery. Cook 8 min until soft.
  2. Add the minced garlic, rosemary, and chilli flakes (if using). Stir 30 sec.
  3. Tip in half the beans. Stir 1 min. With a fork or potato masher, mash these beans roughly against the side of the pot — they'll thicken the broth.
  4. Add the tinned tomatoes, vegetable stock, and parmesan rind (if you have one — fish out at the end).
  5. Bring to a simmer. Cook uncovered 10 min.
  6. Add the remaining whole beans and the pasta. Simmer 8-10 min until pasta is just tender. Stir occasionally — pasta tends to sink and stick.
  7. Off the heat, stir in a final glug of olive oil. Taste — adjust salt and pepper. Should be thick enough that pasta and beans don't swim freely.
  8. Ladle into bowls. Top with grated parmesan and a grind of pepper. Serve with crusty bread.
