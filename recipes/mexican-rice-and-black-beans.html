Mexican Rice & Black Beans

Tomato-stained rice and earthy black beans — the perfect side, or a hearty vegan main on its own.

30 minEasy~575 kcal / servingMexican
vegetarianvegangluten freedairy free

Ingredients · per serving

  • 150 ml Vegetable stock
  • 1 wedge Lime
  • 1 clove Garlic
  • 1.5 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 120 g Black beans
  • 70 g Long-grain rice
  • small handful Fresh coriander
  • 1 tbsp Tomato paste
  • to taste Salt
  • ½ tsp Smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp Garlic powder
  • 40 g Onion
