Halloumi & Mediterranean Veg Traybake

Halloumi slabs roasted with cherry tomatoes, peppers, courgette and red onion in olive oil, oregano and lemon. Vegetarian sheet-pan dinner with serious flavour for minimal effort.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 2 tsp Dried oregano
  • 150 ml Vegetable stock
  • 1 medium Lemon
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley
  • 1 medium Courgette
  • 1 medium Red bell pepper
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 4 tbsp Olive oil
  • 250 g Halloumi
  • 80 g Couscous
  • 1 medium Yellow bell pepper
  • 250 g Cherry tomatoes
  • 1 medium Red onion
  • to taste Salt
  • to taste Fresh basil
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C/200°C fan. Cube the courgette, peppers and red onion into 2cm chunks. Tip onto a large baking tray. Add the whole garlic cloves.
  2. Drizzle with 3 tbsp olive oil. Sprinkle with dried oregano, smoked paprika, salt and pepper. Toss to coat.
  3. Roast 15 min — gives the veg a head start.
  4. Add the cherry tomatoes to the tray, toss in.
  5. Slice the halloumi into 1cm slabs. After tomatoes have been in for 5 min, lay the halloumi slabs on top of the veg.
  6. Roast 10-12 more minutes until the halloumi is deep gold and the tomatoes have burst.
  7. Squeeze in the lemon juice. Tip the entire tray over warm couscous (cook 80g couscous in 150ml boiling stock for 5 min, fluff with fork). Scatter fresh basil, parsley, a final drizzle of olive oil and a lemon wedge.
