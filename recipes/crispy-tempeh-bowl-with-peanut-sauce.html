Crispy Tempeh Bowl with Peanut Sauce

Pan-fried tempeh strips with a glossy peanut-soy-lime sauce, over jasmine rice with garlicky greens, carrots and crispy shallots. Indonesian-leaning vegan bowl with deep umami.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 medium Lime
  • 1 medium Carrot
  • 1 tbsp Roasted peanuts (unsalted)
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 200 g Tempeh
  • 2.5 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • 1 tbsp Crispy fried shallots
  • 3 tbsp Peanut butter
  • 80 ml Coconut milk
  • ½ tsp Sambal oelek
  • 1 tsp Fresh ginger
  • 120 g Pak choi
  • 3 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Light brown sugar
  • 75 g Jasmine rice
  • ½ tsp Garlic powder

Instructions

  1. Rinse the rice. Combine with 150ml water, pinch salt. Boil, cover, lowest heat 12 min, rest 5 min off heat.
  2. Slice the tempeh into 5mm-thick strips. Toss with the soy sauce and a pinch of garlic powder.
  3. Heat 2 tbsp sunflower oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat. Add the tempeh strips in a single layer. Fry 3 minutes per side until deep golden and crispy at the edges. Lift onto kitchen paper.
  4. Make the peanut sauce: whisk peanut butter, coconut milk, soy sauce, lime juice, brown sugar, minced garlic, grated ginger and chili paste (if using) in a small pan. Warm over low heat 2-3 minutes until smooth and glossy. Loosen with water if too thick.
  5. In the same pan you fried the tempeh, add 1 tsp oil, sliced carrot matchsticks. Stir-fry 90 seconds. Add the pak choi or spinach with the minced garlic. Stir-fry 60 seconds until just wilted.
  6. Toss the crispy tempeh into the peanut sauce — coat each piece glossy.
  7. Build the bowls: rice as base, pile on the tempeh, carrots and greens. Drizzle any remaining peanut sauce over. Top with crispy fried shallots, sliced spring onion, chopped peanuts and a lime wedge.
