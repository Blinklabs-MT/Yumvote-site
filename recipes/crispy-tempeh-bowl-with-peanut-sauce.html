Crispy Tempeh Bowl with Peanut Sauce

Pan-fried tempeh strips with a glossy peanut-soy-lime sauce, over jasmine rice with garlicky greens, carrots and crispy shallots. Indonesian-leaning vegan bowl with deep umami.

30 minEasy~697 kcal / servingIndonesian
Contains soyContains nuts

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 medium Lime
  • 1 medium Carrot
  • 1 tbsp Roasted peanuts (unsalted)
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 200 g Tempeh
  • 2.5 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • 1 tbsp Crispy fried shallots
  • 3 tbsp Peanut butter
  • 80 ml Coconut milk
  • ½ tsp Sambal oelek
  • 1 tsp Fresh ginger
  • 120 g Pak choi
  • 3 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Light brown sugar
  • 75 g Jasmine rice
  • ½ tsp Garlic powder
