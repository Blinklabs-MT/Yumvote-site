Country Captain Chicken

Southern-Indian fusion stew of chicken, sultanas, almonds, peppers and curry spices in tomato-coconut sauce, over rice. Charleston colonial classic — the Indian Ocean meets the Carolina coast.

60 minMedium~562 kcal / servingAmerican
Contains glutenContains nuts

  • 1 medium Green bell pepper

  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 3 tbsp Mango chutney
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 100 ml Coconut milk
  • ¼ tsp Cayenne pepper
  • 2 tbsp Mild curry powder
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 30 g Sliced almonds
  • 60 g Sultanas
  • 2 tbsp Tomato purée

  • 3 tbsp Fresh coriander
  • 1 tsp Dried thyme

  • 400 g Tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 4 thighs Chicken thigh
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 large Onion
