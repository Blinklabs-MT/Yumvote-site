Coconut Chickpea Curry

Chickpeas simmered in a tomato-coconut sauce with garlic, ginger and warm spices, served over rice with naan. 25-minute vegan curry that doesn't taste like a compromise.

25 minEasy~554 kcal / servingIndian
Contains gluten

Ingredients · per serving

  • ½ medium Lemon
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 2 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • 1 tsp Ground coriander
  • 250 ml Coconut milk
  • 1 small Green chili
  • 1 tsp Garam masala
  • 2 tsp Fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 tbsp Tomato purée
  • to taste Fresh coriander
  • 1 small Naan
  • 400 g Tinned chickpeas
  • to taste Salt
  • 60 g Baby spinach
  • 200 g Tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 75 g Jasmine rice
  • ½ tsp Smoked paprika
  • ½ tsp Ground turmeric
  • 1 medium Onion
