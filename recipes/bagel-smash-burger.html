Bagel Smash Burger

TikTok's favourite: smashed beef patty, melted cheddar, crispy onions, and burger sauce inside a toasted everything bagel.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 2 slices Cheddar cheese slices
  • 1 tbsp Mayonnaise
  • 1 tsp Ketchup
  • 3 slices Dill pickles
  • 1 bagel Everything bagel
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 1 tsp Butter
  • 130 g Beef mince
  • to taste Salt
  • ½ tsp Yellow mustard
  • 40 g Onion

Instructions

  1. Slice the bagel. Spread butter on the cut sides and toast in a hot pan until golden. Set aside.
  2. Form beef into a loose ball, slightly bigger than the bagel hole.
  3. Heat the same pan over high heat with sliced onion + pinch of salt. Cook 4 min stirring, until deeply browned and crispy at edges. Push to one side.
  4. Drop the beef ball into the hot pan. Smash HARD with a spatula to about 1cm thick. Season top with salt + pepper. Cook 90 sec without moving.
  5. Flip, lay cheese on top, cook 45 sec. The cheese should be fully melted and dripping.
  6. Mix mayo + ketchup + mustard + tiny dash pickle juice for burger sauce. Spread on both bagel halves.
  7. Build: bottom bagel → patty (with cheese) → crispy onions → pickles → top bagel. Smash down gently.
