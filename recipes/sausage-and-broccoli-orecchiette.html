Sausage & Broccoli Orecchiette

Italian sausagemeat crisped in olive oil, tossed through ear-shaped pasta and tenderstem broccoli with garlic, chili and parmesan. The 20-minute weeknight pasta that tastes like a trattoria.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 100 g Orecchiette
  • to taste Black pepper
  • ½ tsp Chili flakes
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 30 g Parmesan
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 150 g Tenderstem broccoli
  • to taste Salt
  • 200 g Italian sausages

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to the boil. Drop in the orecchiette and cook to packet time (usually 10-12 minutes).
  2. While the pasta cooks, slice the broccoli into bite-sized florets, halving the stems if thick. Squeeze the sausagemeat out of the casings.
  3. Heat the olive oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat. Add the sausagemeat in chunks. Brown 5-6 minutes, breaking up with a wooden spoon, until deeply crisp at the edges.
  4. Lower heat to medium. Add the minced garlic and chili flakes, stir 30 seconds — careful not to burn the garlic.
  5. Add the broccoli florets to the boiling pasta water for the last 3 minutes of cooking — they finish at the same time as the pasta.
  6. Scoop a mug of pasta water out. Drain the pasta and broccoli together. Tip into the sausage pan with a splash of pasta water.
  7. Toss vigorously over high heat for 1 minute — the starch in the water emulsifies into a glossy sauce that coats every piece. Grate parmesan over the top. Plate, more parmesan, a glug of olive oil.
