Roasted Veg & Halloumi Couscous Bowl

Fluffy couscous topped with charred peppers, courgette, red onion, golden halloumi, and lemon-herb dressing.

30 minEasy~366 kcal / servingMediterranean
Contains glutenContains dairyvegetarian

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • ½ lemon Lemon
  • 1 small courgette Courgette
  • to taste Black pepper
  • small handful Fresh mint or parsley
  • 2.5 tbsp Olive oil
  • 100 g Halloumi
  • 60 g Couscous
  • 1 pepper Red pepper
  • 1 small onion Red onion
  • to taste Salt
