Thai Coconut Chicken Soup (Tom Kha Gai)

Coconut-rich Thai soup with chicken, mushrooms, ginger, and lime. Aromatic, soothing, ready in 25 min.

25 minMedium~344 kcal / servingAsian
Contains fishgluten freedairy free

Ingredients · per serving

  • 300 ml Chicken stock
  • ½ lime Lime
  • 1.5 tbsp Fish sauce
  • 80 g Mushrooms
  • 200 ml Coconut milk
  • 4 thin coins Fresh ginger
  • 60 g Cherry tomatoes
  • small handful Fresh coriander
  • 1 onion Spring onion
  • slices Red chilli
  • 140 g Chicken thigh
  • 3 leaves Lime leaves (kaffir)
  • ½ tsp Sugar
  • 1 stalk Lemongrass
