Chorizo & White Bean Skillet

Smoky chorizo crisped in a pan with white beans, tomatoes, and spinach. Pantry hero, 20 minutes flat.

20 minEasy~573 kcal / servingMediterranean

Ingredients · per serving

  • ¼ lemon Lemon
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 80 g Cooking chorizo
  • to taste Salt
  • 60 g Baby spinach
  • 200 g Tinned chopped tomatoes
  • ½ tsp Smoked paprika
  • 200 g Tinned white beans
  • 1 small onion Onion

Instructions

  1. Slice the chorizo into 5mm coins. Mince the garlic.
  2. Heat a large frying pan over medium-high heat (no oil — chorizo releases its own).
  3. Add the chorizo. Cook 4 min, stirring, until it's crisp at the edges and has released bright red-orange oil.
  4. Add the chopped onion. Cook 4 min until soft.
  5. Add the garlic and smoked paprika. Stir 30 sec.
  6. Tip in the drained beans and the chopped tomatoes. Stir. Bring to a simmer. Cook 6 min until thick and saucy.
  7. Add the spinach in handfuls, stirring until it wilts (2 min).
  8. Squeeze in the lemon juice. Taste — adjust salt (chorizo is salty, you may not need much).
  9. Serve in a bowl with crusty bread to mop up the juices. Or over rice / mashed potato.
