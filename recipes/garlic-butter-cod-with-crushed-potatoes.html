Garlic Butter Cod with Crushed Potatoes

Cod fillets pan-fried in foaming garlic butter with capers, parsley and lemon, served over crushed buttery new potatoes. British seaside, 25 minutes in your kitchen.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 medium Lemon
  • 80 g Frozen peas
  • 3 tbsp Fresh parsley
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 40 g Butter
  • 80 g Cherry tomatoes
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tbsp Capers
  • 250 g Cod fillet
  • 300 g New potatoes

Instructions

  1. Halve the new potatoes. Boil in salted water 12-15 minutes until knife-tender.
  2. While the potatoes boil, pat the cod fillets dry. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
  3. Drain the potatoes well, return to the hot pan. Add the butter, salt and chopped parsley. Crush each potato lightly with the back of a fork — they should be smashed, not mashed.
  4. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a non-stick pan over medium-high heat. Lay in the cod fillets. Don't move for 3 minutes — let the crust form.
  5. Flip the cod gently. Add 25g butter to the pan. As it foams, add the minced garlic, capers and a small handful of cherry tomatoes.
  6. Tilt the pan and spoon the foaming garlic-caper butter over the fish for 1 minute. The cod is done when it flakes easily — about 5 minutes total.
  7. Plate the crushed potatoes. Lay the cod on top. Spoon the pan butter, capers and burst tomatoes over. Scatter parsley, squeeze with lemon. Serve with steamed greens (peas, broccoli) alongside.
