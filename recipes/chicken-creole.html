Chicken Creole

Chicken pieces simmered in a tomato-based Creole sauce with the holy trinity, garlic, herbs and cayenne, over rice. Louisiana lighter cousin to gumbo — same trinity, more tomato, fewer hours.

45 minEasy~527 kcal / servingAmerican

Ingredients · per serving

  • 300 ml Chicken stock
  • 1 medium Green bell pepper
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 2 leaves Bay leaf
  • 1 medium Lemon
  • 3 tbsp Fresh parsley
  • 2 stalks Celery
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 5 cloves Garlic
  • 500 g Chicken thigh fillets
  • 1 tbsp Cajun seasoning
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • to taste Hot sauce
  • ½ tsp Cayenne pepper
  • 1 tbsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 2 tbsp Tomato purée
  • 150 g Long-grain rice
  • 1 tsp Dried thyme
  • 2 stalks Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 400 g Tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 large Onion

Instructions

  1. Cube the chicken thighs into 3cm pieces. Season with salt, pepper, smoked paprika and cayenne.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a wide heavy pan over medium-high heat. Brown the chicken in 2 batches, 4 min per batch. Lift onto a plate.
  3. Lower heat to medium. Add the diced onion, celery and bell pepper to the pan. Cook 6 min until soft.
  4. Add minced garlic, Creole seasoning (or Cajun), dried thyme, dried oregano and bay leaves. Stir 60 seconds.
  5. Stir in tomato purée, cook 1 minute. Add the tinned tomatoes, chicken stock, Worcestershire sauce.
  6. Return the chicken with plate juices. Bring to a simmer, lower heat, cover slightly, cook 20-25 minutes until the chicken is tender and the sauce has thickened.
  7. Adjust salt and pepper. Squeeze in a wedge of lemon. Stir in chopped parsley.
  8. Cook the rice separately: rinse, combine with 300ml water + pinch salt. Boil, cover, lowest heat 12 min, rest 5 min. Ladle the chicken Creole generously over the rice. Scatter sliced spring onion. Hot sauce on the side.
