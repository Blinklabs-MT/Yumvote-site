Lentil & Spinach Protein Curry

Green lentils + spinach + tomato in a gently spiced curry sauce. Plant-based, high-protein, high-fibre, kitchen-pantry-only.

35 minEasy~502 kcal / servingIndian

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 tbsp Coconut oil
  • 200 ml Vegetable stock
  • ¼ lemon Lemon
  • slices Green chilli
  • 80 g Basmati rice
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tsp Ground coriander
  • ½ tsp Garam masala
  • 1 tsp grated Fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • small handful Fresh coriander
  • 1 tbsp Tomato paste
  • to taste Salt
  • 100 g Baby spinach
  • 200 g Tinned chopped tomatoes
  • ½ tsp Ground turmeric
  • 240 g Tinned green or brown lentils
  • 1 onion Onion

Instructions

  1. Heat 1 tbsp coconut oil (or olive oil) in a pot over medium heat. Add chopped onion. Cook 5 min until soft.
  2. Add minced garlic, grated ginger, and chilli (if using). Cook 1 min.
  3. Add ground cumin, ground coriander, garam masala, ground turmeric. Stir 30 sec.
  4. Add the tomato paste, stir 1 min. Add the tinned tomatoes and 200ml stock or water. Stir.
  5. Add the drained green lentils. Bring to a simmer. Cover. Cook 15 min — flavours marry and sauce thickens.
  6. Stir in the spinach in handfuls until wilted (2 min).
  7. Off the heat, squeeze in lemon juice. Taste — adjust salt.
  8. Meanwhile, cook the basmati rice (rinse first, simmer covered 12 min, off heat 5 min).
  9. Scatter chopped coriander over the curry. Serve over rice.
