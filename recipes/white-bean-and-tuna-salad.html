White Bean & Tuna Salad

Tinned cannellini beans with flaked tuna, red onion, parsley and a lemon-Dijon vinaigrette. Italian-Tuscan pantry salad — meal-sized, ready in 10 minutes flat.

Ingredients · per serving

  • ½ medium Cucumber
  • 1 medium Lemon
  • 4 tbsp Fresh parsley
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 1 clove Garlic
  • 400 g Tinned cannellini beans
  • 4 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 slices Sourdough bread
  • 150 g Cherry tomatoes
  • 80 g Mixed salad leaves
  • 160 g Tuna in olive oil
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • ½ medium Red onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tbsp Capers

Instructions

  1. Drain and rinse the cannellini beans well. Tip into a wide bowl.
  2. Drain the tuna, gently flake into the bowl with the beans.
  3. Slice the red onion very thinly. Soak in cold water for 5 minutes — takes the sharp edge off. Drain and pat dry.
  4. Make the dressing: whisk lemon juice, Dijon mustard, minced garlic, olive oil, salt and pepper in a small bowl.
  5. Halve the cherry tomatoes. Slice the cucumber. Roughly chop the parsley.
  6. Add the red onion, tomatoes, cucumber, capers and parsley to the bean-tuna bowl.
  7. Pour the dressing over. Toss gently — keep the tuna chunks visible, don't crush.
  8. Plate over a handful of mixed leaves. Drizzle with extra olive oil. A lemon wedge and a slice of toasted sourdough alongside. Sprinkle of parmesan if you like.
