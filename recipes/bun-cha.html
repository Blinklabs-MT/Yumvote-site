Bun Cha

Vietnamese street food — sweet-glazed grilled pork meatballs over cool rice noodles, with herbs, lettuce and a nuoc cham dipping sauce. Bright, light, addictive, 30 minutes.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 medium Lime
  • ½ medium Carrot
  • 2.5 tbsp Fish sauce
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tsp Sunflower oil
  • 4 leaves Little gem lettuce
  • 1 tbsp Honey
  • 1 tsp Soy sauce
  • 1 small bunch Fresh mint
  • 1 small Shallot
  • 1 tsp Cornflour
  • 1 small bunch Fresh coriander
  • 2 tbsp Light brown sugar
  • 100 g Rice vermicelli noodles
  • 250 g Pork mince
  • 1 small Red chili

Instructions

  1. Mix the pork mince with the minced shallot, garlic, fish sauce, sugar, black pepper and cornflour. Knead for 30 seconds to develop the bind.
  2. Shape into 12 small meatballs (about 20g each). Wet your hands so the mix doesn't stick.
  3. Cook the rice noodles to packet time (usually 3-4 minutes in boiling water). Drain, rinse under cold water, set aside.
  4. Mix the nuoc cham: fish sauce, lime juice, water, sugar, minced garlic and chopped red chili in a small bowl. Stir until the sugar dissolves. Add the matchstick carrot to marinate.
  5. Heat a griddle pan over high heat, brush with oil. Add the meatballs. Cook 3 minutes per side, pressing slightly to flatten, until charred outside and just cooked through.
  6. Brush the meatballs with the honey-soy glaze (honey + soy + 1 tsp water mixed) for the last 30 seconds. Lift onto a plate.
  7. Plate: noodles in shallow bowls, meatballs on top, pile herb leaves (mint, coriander, basil) and lettuce alongside. Spoon the nuoc cham over the meatballs and noodles. Eat by mixing everything together.
