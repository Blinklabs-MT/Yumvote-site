Smoked Salmon & Whipped Cream Cheese Bagel

Toasted everything bagel with whipped lemon-dill cream cheese, smoked salmon, red onion, capers, cucumber and tomato.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 40 g Cucumber
  • ½ lemon Lemon
  • 3 tbsp Cream cheese
  • ½ tomato Tomato
  • 1 bagel Everything bagel
  • to taste Black pepper
  • small handful Fresh dill
  • 15 g Red onion
  • pinch Salt
  • 1 tsp Capers
  • 80 g Smoked salmon

Instructions

  1. Slice + toast the bagel.
  2. Whip cream cheese with lemon zest + chopped dill + tiny pinch salt + pepper.
  3. Slice cucumber rounds, red onion paper-thin, tomato thin.
  4. Spread whipped cream cheese on both bagel halves.
  5. Layer: cucumber → smoked salmon → tomato → red onion → capers.
  6. Pepper. Lemon squeeze. Sit a fresh dill sprig on top.
