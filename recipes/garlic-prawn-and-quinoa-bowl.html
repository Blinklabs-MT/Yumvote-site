Garlic Prawn & Quinoa Bowl

Garlic-butter prawns over warm quinoa with charred broccoli, cherry tomatoes and avocado, finished with a lemon-chili drizzle. Restaurant-style protein bowl, 25 minutes.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 250 g Raw king prawns (peeled)
  • 1 medium Lemon
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley
  • ½ medium Avocado
  • to taste Black pepper
  • ½ tsp Chili flakes
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • 75 g Quinoa
  • 150 g Broccoli florets
  • 25 g Butter
  • 120 g Cherry tomatoes
  • to taste Salt

Instructions

  1. Rinse the quinoa. Combine with 150ml water, pinch salt. Boil, cover, lowest heat 12 min, rest 5 min off heat. Fluff with a fork.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a wide pan over high heat. Add the broccoli florets and a pinch of salt. Don't move for 2 minutes — let the bottoms char. Stir and cook 3 minutes more until tender-crisp with charred edges. Lift onto a plate.
  3. In the same pan, add the cherry tomatoes. Toss for 2 minutes until they blister and burst. Lift onto the plate.
  4. Wipe the pan. Melt the butter over medium-high heat. Add the minced garlic and chili flakes. Stir 30 seconds — careful not to burn.
  5. Add the prawns. Cook 90 seconds per side until pink and curled. Squeeze in the lemon juice, off the heat.
  6. Make the drizzle: whisk lemon juice, olive oil, chili flakes, minced garlic, salt and pepper.
  7. Build the bowls: quinoa as base. Top with the prawns and pan butter, charred broccoli, burst tomatoes and sliced avocado. Drizzle with the lemon-chili oil. Scatter parsley, lemon wedge.
