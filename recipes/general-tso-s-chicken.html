General Tso's Chicken

Crispy battered chicken in a glossy sweet-spicy-tangy sauce with garlic and chili. The American-Chinese takeaway classic, demystified and ready in 30 minutes.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 4 small Dried red chilis
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 200 g Chicken thigh fillets
  • 100 ml Sunflower oil
  • 1 white only Egg
  • 2 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp Fresh ginger
  • 3 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 80 g Broccoli florets
  • 4 tbsp Cornflour
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tbsp Hoisin sauce
  • 2 tbsp Light brown sugar
  • 75 g Jasmine rice

Instructions

  1. Rinse the rice in cold water. Combine with 150ml water, pinch of salt. Bring to boil, cover, lowest heat 12 min, rest 5 min off heat.
  2. Cube the chicken into 2cm pieces. Toss with 1 tbsp soy sauce, the egg white and cornflour until each piece is coated.
  3. Mix the sauce: rice vinegar, soy sauce, brown sugar, hoisin, water and the cornflour slurry in a small bowl.
  4. Heat 2cm sunflower oil in a wok or wide pan over medium-high heat. When a piece of chicken sizzles immediately, fry in 2 batches for 4 minutes per batch until deep golden and crisp. Lift onto kitchen paper.
  5. Pour out all but 1 tbsp of the oil. Add the dried chilis and ginger, stir 30 seconds (don't let the chilis burn).
  6. Add the minced garlic, stir 30 seconds. Pour in the sauce mix. It'll bubble and thicken in 60 seconds.
  7. Tip the crispy chicken back in. Toss to coat in the glossy sauce. Off the heat immediately. Plate over the rice, scatter with sliced spring onion and steamed broccoli alongside.
