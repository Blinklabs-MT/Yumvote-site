Chickpea & Spinach Pasta with Lemon

Pasta tossed with chickpeas, wilted spinach, garlic, chili and lemon — fast, satisfying and almost entirely from the pantry. Italian peasant cooking, 20 minutes flat.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 medium Lemon
  • to taste Black pepper
  • ½ tsp Chili flakes
  • 200 g Spaghetti
  • 4 cloves Garlic
  • 40 g Parmesan
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • 400 g Tinned chickpeas
  • to taste Salt
  • to taste Fresh basil
  • 120 g Baby spinach

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a rolling boil. Drop in the pasta and cook to packet time.
  2. Drain and rinse the chickpeas. Heat the olive oil in a wide pan over medium heat. Add the minced garlic and chili flakes. Stir 30 seconds — careful, garlic burns fast.
  3. Add the chickpeas. Cook 5 minutes, stirring, until warmed through and starting to crisp at the edges.
  4. Scoop a mug of pasta water. Add half to the chickpea pan. The starch makes a glossy sauce.
  5. Add the baby spinach in handfuls. Toss as it wilts down (60 seconds).
  6. Drain the pasta and tip into the pan with a splash more pasta water. Toss vigorously for 30 seconds — the starch emulsifies everything.
  7. Off the heat, stir in lemon juice, lemon zest, grated parmesan and a generous grind of black pepper. Plate, drizzle olive oil, scatter more parmesan and a few torn basil leaves.
