Chicken Cacciatore

Italian hunter's chicken — thighs braised with onions, peppers, garlic, olives and tomato in a single pan. Rustic, herby, ready in 30 minutes when you use chicken thighs. Serve over polenta or pasta.

30 minMedium~553 kcal / servingItalian
Contains gluten

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • to taste Fresh parsley
  • 1 medium Red bell pepper
  • 80 ml White wine
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 250 g Chicken thigh fillets
  • 50 g Kalamata olives
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 100 g Pasta (small shape)
  • to taste Salt
  • 200 g Tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 1 medium Onion
