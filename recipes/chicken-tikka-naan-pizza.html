Chicken Tikka Naan Pizza

Warm naan bread topped with garlicky tikka chicken, melted mozzarella, red onion and a swirl of mango chutney. Indian-Italian crossover, ready in 25 minutes — the fakeaway that beats both takeaway origins.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 medium Lemon
  • 1 medium Lime
  • 2 large Naan bread
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 300 g Chicken thigh fillets
  • 3 tbsp Mango chutney
  • 1 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • 150 g Mozzarella
  • 80 g Greek yogurt
  • ½ tsp Ground cumin
  • 3 tbsp Tikka paste
  • 1 small bunch Fresh coriander
  • ½ medium Red onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 80 g Passata
  • ½ small Red chili

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 220°C/200°C fan. Place a baking tray on the top shelf to heat up.
  2. Cube the chicken thighs into 2cm pieces. Toss with tikka paste, yogurt, lemon juice, salt and pepper. Let marinate while you prep (5 min minimum).
  3. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat. Add the marinated chicken. Cook 5-6 minutes, stirring, until cooked through with charred edges.
  4. Mix the sauce: passata with 1 tsp tikka paste, minced garlic, ground cumin, salt and pepper.
  5. Lay the naans on a sheet of baking parchment. Spread each with a thin layer of sauce, leaving a 1cm border.
  6. Scatter half the torn mozzarella over each. Distribute the tikka chicken evenly. Top with thinly sliced red onion and a few coriander leaves.
  7. Carefully transfer (with parchment) onto the hot baking tray in the oven. Bake 8-10 minutes until the cheese is melted and bubbly and the naan crust is crisp.
  8. Lift onto a board. Drizzle each pizza with mango chutney in zigzags. Scatter fresh coriander, sliced red chili (if you like) and a small dollop of yogurt. Slice into 4 wedges. Lime wedge.
