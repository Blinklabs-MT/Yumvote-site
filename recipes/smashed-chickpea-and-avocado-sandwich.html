Smashed Chickpea & Avocado Sandwich

Lemony smashed chickpeas folded with avocado, red onion, celery + dill — like a vegan tuna salad — on sourdough with greens.

Ingredients · per serving

  • ½ lemon Lemon
  • small handful Fresh parsley
  • 1 avocado Avocado
  • ½ tomato Tomato
  • 30 g Celery
  • to taste Black pepper
  • small handful Fresh dill
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 slices Sourdough bread
  • 20 g Mixed salad leaves
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 240 g Tinned chickpeas
  • 30 g Red onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tsp Capers

Instructions

  1. Drain chickpeas. Tip into a bowl with avocado. Smash with a fork — keep some chunky.
  2. Add finely diced red onion + celery + chopped dill + parsley + capers + dijon + lemon juice + olive oil + salt + pepper.
  3. Fold together. Should be creamy from the avocado + chunky from chickpeas.
  4. Toast sourdough.
  5. Layer: bottom slice → baby leaves → smash mix → tomato slices → top slice.
  6. Press, cut in half. Best eaten same day.
