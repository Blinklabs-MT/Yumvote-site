Honey Sriracha Salmon Bowl

Glossy honey-sriracha glazed salmon over rice with cucumber, avocado, scallion, and a sesame-soy drizzle.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 80 g Cucumber
  • 150 g Salmon fillet (skinless)
  • 1 tsp Toasted sesame seeds
  • ½ avocado Avocado
  • 120 g Cooked rice
  • 1 clove Garlic
  • 1 tsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp Honey
  • 1 tbsp Soy sauce
  • small pile Pickled ginger
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • pinch Salt
  • 1 tbsp Sriracha
  • pinch Sugar

Instructions

  1. Whisk honey + sriracha + soy + grated garlic + sesame oil for the glaze.
  2. Pat salmon dry. Brush HALF the glaze over the salmon. Bake at 220°C for 8-10 min (depending on thickness) until just flaking but still glossy in the middle.
  3. Meanwhile slice cucumber into ribbons, avocado into wedges, scallion into thin diagonals.
  4. Toss cooked rice with rice vinegar + pinch sugar + pinch salt to make it sushi-style sticky.
  5. Bowl: rice → salmon (flake roughly with a fork) → cucumber → avocado → scallion. Drizzle the remaining glaze over everything.
  6. Finish with sesame seeds + a small dab of extra sriracha for the camera + spoon of pickled ginger if you have it.
