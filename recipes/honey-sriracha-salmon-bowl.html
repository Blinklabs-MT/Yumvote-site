Honey Sriracha Salmon Bowl

Glossy honey-sriracha glazed salmon over rice with cucumber, avocado, scallion, and a sesame-soy drizzle.

20 minEasy~401 kcal / servingSoul Food
Contains fishContains soyContains sesamedairy freepescatarian

Ingredients · per serving

  • 80 g Cucumber
  • 150 g Salmon fillet (skinless)
  • 1 tsp Toasted sesame seeds
  • ½ avocado Avocado
  • 120 g Cooked rice
  • 1 clove Garlic
  • 1 tsp Rice vinegar
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 2 tbsp Honey
  • 1 tbsp Soy sauce
  • small pile Pickled ginger
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • pinch Salt
  • 1 tbsp Sriracha
  • pinch Sugar
