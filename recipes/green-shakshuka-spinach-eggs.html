Green Shakshuka (Spinach + Eggs)

Eggs poached in a vibrant green sauce of spinach, leek, herbs, garlic and goat cheese — cleaner cousin of the classic.

25 minMedium~378 kcal / servingBright Plates

Ingredients · per serving

  • 50 ml Vegetable stock
  • ½ lemon Lemon
  • small handful Fresh parsley
  • 80 g Soft goat cheese
  • to taste Black pepper
  • pinch Chilli flakes
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • small handful Fresh dill
  • 2 tbsp Olive oil
  • 4 eggs Egg
  • ½ tsp Ground cumin
  • 1 leek Leek
  • to taste Salt
  • 300 g Baby spinach
  • to serve Crusty bread

Instructions

  1. Heat olive oil in a wide skillet. Add sliced leek + garlic, cook 5 min until soft.
  2. Add cumin + chili flakes, stir 30 sec.
  3. Add spinach in batches, wilting between additions. Add chopped dill + parsley + splash of stock.
  4. Stir in soft goat cheese to melt slightly into the green sauce.
  5. Make 4 wells, crack eggs in. Cover, cook 4-5 min until whites set, yolks runny.
  6. Top with extra herbs + lemon. Serve with crusty bread.
