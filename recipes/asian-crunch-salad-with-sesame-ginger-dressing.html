Asian Crunch Salad with Sesame Ginger Dressing

Shredded cabbage, carrot, edamame, mango and crispy chow mein noodles tossed in a creamy sesame-ginger dressing — peak crunch.

20 minEasy~385 kcal / servingBright Plates

Ingredients · per serving

  • 100 g Red cabbage
  • 150 g White cabbage
  • 1 lime Lime
  • 1 tsp Toasted sesame seeds
  • 80 g Carrot
  • 1 clove Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Honey or maple
  • 80 g Ripe mango
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 60 g Edamame (shelled)
  • 20 g Cashews
  • 1 tbsp Fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 1.5 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Tahini
  • 30 g Crispy chow mein noodles
  • small handful Fresh coriander
  • 2 stalks Spring onion

Instructions

  1. Shred cabbage thin (red + white if you have both for colour). Julienne carrot. Cube mango. Slice scallion.
  2. Whisk dressing: tahini (or peanut butter) + soy + rice vinegar + sesame oil + grated ginger + minced garlic + honey/maple + lime juice + 2 tbsp warm water. Should be pourable but creamy.
  3. In a big bowl combine cabbage + carrot + edamame + mango + scallion + coriander.
  4. Pour HALF the dressing over, toss vigorously.
  5. Top with crispy noodles + sesame seeds + cashews + extra coriander.
  6. Serve remaining dressing on the side. Eat fast — noodles soften in 10 min.
