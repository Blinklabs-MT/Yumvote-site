Asian Crunch Salad with Sesame Ginger Dressing

Shredded cabbage, carrot, edamame, mango and crispy chow mein noodles tossed in a creamy sesame-ginger dressing — peak crunch.

20 minEasy~385 kcal / servingBright Plates
Contains glutenContains soyContains sesameContains nutsveganvegetariandairy free

Ingredients · per serving

  • 100 g Red cabbage
  • 150 g White cabbage
  • 1 lime Lime
  • 1 tsp Toasted sesame seeds
  • 80 g Carrot
  • 1 clove Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Honey or maple
  • 80 g Ripe mango
  • 1 tbsp Rice vinegar
  • 60 g Edamame (shelled)
  • 20 g Cashews
  • 1 tbsp Fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp Sesame oil
  • 1.5 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 2 tbsp Tahini
  • 30 g Crispy chow mein noodles
  • small handful Fresh coriander
  • 2 stalks Spring onion
