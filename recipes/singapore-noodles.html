Singapore Noodles

Curry-spiced rice vermicelli with prawns, chicken, and crunchy vegetables. Not actually from Singapore — invented in Hong Kong — but completely delicious.

25 minMedium~580 kcal / servingAsian
Contains shellfishContains eggsContains soyContains sesame

Ingredients · per serving

  • 60 g Raw prawns (peeled)
  • 80 g Rice vermicelli
  • 60 g Chicken breast
  • 40 g Beansprouts
  • 2 tbsp Vegetable oil
  • 1 large Egg
  • ½ whole Bell pepper
  • ½ tsp Sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp Mild curry powder
  • 1 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 whole Spring onion
  • ½ tsp Sugar
