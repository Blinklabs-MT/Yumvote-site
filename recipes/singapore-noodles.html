Singapore Noodles

Curry-spiced rice vermicelli with prawns, chicken, and crunchy vegetables. Not actually from Singapore — invented in Hong Kong — but completely delicious.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 60 g Raw prawns (peeled)
  • 1 tbsp Curry powder
  • 60 g Chicken breast
  • 40 g Beansprouts
  • 2 tbsp Vegetable oil
  • 1 large Egg
  • ½ whole Bell pepper
  • ½ tsp Sesame oil
  • 1 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 whole Spring onion
  • 80 g Rice vermicelli noodles
  • ½ tsp Sugar

Instructions

  1. Place the rice vermicelli in a large bowl. Cover with hot tap water and leave for 5 minutes to soften, then drain.
  2. In a small bowl mix the soy sauce, mild curry powder, sesame oil, and sugar into a thick paste.
  3. Slice the chicken into thin strips. Slice the pepper into matchsticks. Halve the prawns lengthways if they're large.
  4. Heat a wok over high heat. Add 1 tablespoon of the oil.
  5. Crack in the egg. Scramble quickly with a spatula until just set, then tip onto a plate.
  6. Add the remaining oil. Stir-fry the chicken for 2-3 minutes until cooked through. Add the prawns, stir-fry 1 minute until pink. Push everything to one side.
  7. Add the pepper, beansprouts, and spring onion whites to the empty side. Stir-fry 1 minute — keep them crisp.
  8. Add the drained vermicelli and the curry paste. Toss with tongs for 2 minutes, lifting and folding, until everything is evenly coated. The noodles will turn yellow.
  9. Return the scrambled egg, toss once more. Plate, scatter spring onion greens on top, serve.
