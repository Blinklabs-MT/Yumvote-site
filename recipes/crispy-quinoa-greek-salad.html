Crispy Quinoa Greek Salad

Pan-toasted quinoa with cucumber, cherry tomato, red onion, olives, feta and a lemony oregano dressing.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 100 g Cucumber
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 1 lemon Lemon
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 1 clove Garlic
  • small handful Fresh mint or parsley
  • 40 g Kalamata olives
  • 3 tbsp Olive oil
  • 70 g Quinoa
  • 120 g Cherry tomatoes
  • ½ tsp Dijon mustard
  • 40 g Red onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 60 g Feta cheese

Instructions

  1. Cook quinoa in salted water 12 min, drain.
  2. Spread on a tray, dry with paper. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a non-stick pan over medium-high. Add quinoa, press flat, cook 6 min until bottom crisps + golden. Toss + cook 3 min more until crispy throughout.
  3. Chop cucumber + tomato + red onion + olives.
  4. Whisk dressing: lemon juice + olive oil + dried oregano + dijon + minced garlic + salt + pepper.
  5. Toss veg in HALF the dressing in a wide bowl. Top with crispy quinoa.
  6. Crumble feta over. Drizzle remaining dressing. Scatter mint or parsley.
