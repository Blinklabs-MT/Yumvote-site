Pesto Pasta with Cherry Tomatoes & Mozzarella

Cooked pasta tossed warm with pesto, blistered cherry tomatoes, and torn mozzarella. Five ingredients, fifteen minutes.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 90 g Fusilli
  • 3 tbsp Pesto (green)
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 80 g Mozzarella
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 150 g Cherry tomatoes
  • to taste Salt
  • small handful Fresh basil

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of salted water to a boil. Add the pasta. Cook to packet time (usually 10 min for fusilli).
  2. Meanwhile, halve the cherry tomatoes. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a frying pan over medium-high heat.
  3. Add the tomatoes cut-side down. Cook 3 min until they blister and soften. Season with salt.
  4. Just before pasta is done, scoop out 100ml of the pasta water with a mug. Drain the pasta.
  5. Return pasta to the pot (off heat). Add the pesto and a splash of pasta water. Stir vigorously — the water loosens the pesto into a glossy sauce.
  6. Add the warm tomatoes and any juices from the pan. Toss.
  7. Tear the mozzarella into chunks straight into the warm pasta. Don't stir too hard — you want pockets of melty cheese, not a gluey mess.
  8. Scatter basil leaves. Top with a grind of black pepper and a final drizzle of olive oil. Serve straight away.
