Tofu Satay Bowl with Crunchy Veg

Crispy fried tofu in a sticky peanut-coconut satay sauce, over jasmine rice with crunchy raw vegetables, herbs and crushed peanuts. Indonesian-Thai flavours, 25 minutes.

25 minEasy~582 kcal / servingIndonesian
Contains soyContains nuts

Ingredients · per serving

  • 80 g Red cabbage
  • ½ medium Cucumber
  • 1 medium Lime
  • 1 medium Carrot
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 tbsp Roasted peanuts (unsalted)
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 2 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • 1 tbsp Crispy fried shallots
  • 3 tbsp Peanut butter
  • 100 ml Coconut milk
  • ½ tsp Sambal oelek
  • 1 tsp Fresh ginger
  • 2 tbsp Soy sauce
  • 1 small bunch Fresh mint
  • 3 tbsp Cornflour
  • 1 small bunch Fresh coriander
  • 250 g Firm tofu
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 1 tbsp Light brown sugar
  • 75 g Jasmine rice
  • ½ tsp Garlic powder
