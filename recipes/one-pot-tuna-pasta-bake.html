One-Pot Tuna Pasta Bake

Pasta cooked in a creamy tomato-tuna sauce, then topped with cheese and grilled until bubbly. One pot from start to finish.

25 minEasy~549 kcal / servingItalian
Contains glutenContains dairyContains fish

Ingredients · per serving

  • 150 ml Chicken or veg stock
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp Crème fraîche
  • small handful Fresh parsley
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 50 g Tinned sweetcorn
  • 90 g Pasta (small shape)
  • to taste Salt
  • 40 g Cheddar cheese
  • 200 g Tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 80 g Tinned tuna
  • 1 small onion Onion
