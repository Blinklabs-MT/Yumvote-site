One-Pot Tuna Pasta Bake

Pasta cooked in a creamy tomato-tuna sauce, then topped with cheese and grilled until bubbly. One pot from start to finish.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 150 ml Chicken or veg stock
  • 1 tsp Dried oregano
  • 2 tbsp Crème fraîche
  • small handful Fresh parsley
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 50 g Tinned sweetcorn
  • 90 g Pasta (small shape)
  • to taste Salt
  • 40 g Cheddar cheese
  • 200 g Tinned chopped tomatoes
  • 80 g Tinned tuna
  • 1 small onion Onion

Instructions

  1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil in a large oven-safe frying pan (or shallow pot) over medium heat.
  2. Add the chopped onion. Cook 4 min until soft. Add the minced garlic, cook 30 sec.
  3. Add the tinned tomatoes, stock, dried oregano, and pasta. Stir.
  4. Bring to a simmer. Cook uncovered 10-12 min, stirring every 2 min so pasta doesn't stick to the bottom. Add a splash more stock if it gets too dry — it should be saucy.
  5. When pasta is just tender, stir in the drained tuna, sweetcorn, and crème fraîche (or cream cheese). Taste — adjust salt and pepper.
  6. Preheat the grill to high.
  7. Scatter the grated cheese over the top. Slide the pan under the grill 3-4 min until the cheese is bubbly and golden brown in patches.
  8. Rest 2 min (cheese is molten-hot). Scatter parsley. Serve from the pan.
