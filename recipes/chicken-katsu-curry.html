Chicken Katsu Curry

Crispy panko-breaded chicken sliced over rice, smothered in glossy Japanese curry sauce. The pub-meal-turned-takeaway-staple, made better at home in 30 minutes flat.

30 minMedium~706 kcal / servingJapanese
Contains glutenContains dairyContains eggsContains soy

Ingredients · per serving

  • 30 g Plain flour
  • 200 g Chicken breast
  • ½ medium Carrot
  • 100 ml Sunflower oil
  • 40 g Japanese curry roux cubes
  • 1 large Egg
  • 50 g Panko breadcrumbs
  • 1 tsp Soy sauce
  • 15 g Butter
  • 1 stalk Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 75 g Jasmine rice
  • ½ medium Onion
