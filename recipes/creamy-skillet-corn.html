Creamy Skillet Corn

Sweetcorn kernels cooked in a buttery cream sauce with crispy bacon, garlic, jalapeño and a snow of cheddar. American Southern side dish so good it's the meal — eat with crusty bread.

25 minEasy~537 kcal / servingAmerican
Contains dairyContains gluten

Ingredients · per serving

  • to taste Black pepper
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 500 g Sweetcorn
  • 120 g Smoked bacon
  • 120 g Mature cheddar
  • 4 slices Sourdough bread
  • 30 g Butter
  • 1 small Jalapeño
  • 1 tbsp Fresh chives
  • 80 g Mixed salad leaves
  • 2 stalks Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 150 ml Double cream
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 100 ml Whole milk
  • 1 medium Onion
