Mac and Cheese

Tubes of macaroni in a silky cheddar sauce, baked until the top is golden and crispy. American comfort food at its purest.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 2 tbsp Breadcrumbs
  • 25 g Plain flour
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 100 g Macaroni
  • ½ tsp Mustard powder
  • 25 g Butter
  • to taste Salt
  • 80 g Cheddar cheese
  • 250 ml Whole milk

Instructions

  1. Bring a large pot of well-salted water to a boil. Add the macaroni. Cook 1 min less than the packet says — it'll finish cooking in the cheese sauce.
  2. While the pasta cooks, make the cheese sauce: melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat. Whisk in the flour, cook 1 min — this is a "roux", the base of most cream sauces.
  3. SLOWLY pour in the warm milk while whisking constantly. Patience here — slow pouring + constant whisking = smooth sauce. Lumpy = poured too fast.
  4. Simmer 3-4 min, whisking often, until the sauce thickens to the consistency of single cream.
  5. Off the heat, stir in 80% of the grated cheese (save 20% for the top). Add the mustard powder (if using) and a generous grind of black pepper.
  6. Drain the macaroni. Tip into the cheese sauce, stir to coat every tube.
  7. Heat oven to 200°C / 400°F.
  8. Tip the mac and cheese into a small baking dish. Sprinkle the remaining cheese on top, plus the breadcrumbs.
  9. Bake 15-20 min until the top is golden and crispy and the sauce bubbles at the edges. Rest 3 min before serving.
