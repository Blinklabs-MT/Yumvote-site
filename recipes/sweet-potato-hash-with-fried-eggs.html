Sweet Potato Hash with Fried Eggs

Crispy sweet potato cubes with peppers, onion, spinach and chorizo, topped with fried eggs + a sprinkle of feta.

Ingredients · per serving

  • small handful Fresh parsley
  • 250 g Sweet potato
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 eggs Egg
  • few dashes Hot sauce
  • 1 pepper Red pepper
  • 80 g Red onion
  • 60 g Chorizo
  • to taste Salt
  • 30 g Feta cheese
  • 60 g Baby spinach
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika

Instructions

  1. Cube sweet potato 1.5cm. Microwave 4 min to par-cook.
  2. Heat oil in a large skillet. Add chorizo, cook 3 min. Lift out, leave fat.
  3. Add sweet potato to the chorizo fat. Cook 8 min pressing flat until deeply crispy.
  4. Add diced pepper + onion + smoked paprika. Cook 5 min until soft + charring.
  5. Return chorizo. Add spinach + garlic, stir 1 min to wilt.
  6. Push to one side. Crack eggs into the empty side. Fry to liking.
  7. Slide everything onto a plate. Top with crumbled feta + parsley + hot sauce.
