Sweet Potato Hash with Fried Eggs

Crispy sweet potato cubes with peppers, onion, spinach and chorizo, topped with fried eggs + a sprinkle of feta.

30 minEasy~376 kcal / servingBright Plates
Contains dairyContains eggsgluten free

Ingredients · per serving

  • small handful Fresh parsley
  • 250 g Sweet potato
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 2 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 2 eggs Egg
  • few dashes Hot sauce
  • 1 pepper Red pepper
  • 80 g Red onion
  • 60 g Chorizo
  • to taste Salt
  • 30 g Feta cheese
  • 60 g Baby spinach
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
