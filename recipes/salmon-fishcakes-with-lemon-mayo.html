Salmon Fishcakes with Lemon Mayo

Crushed potatoes mixed with flaked salmon, dill and spring onion, shaped into fishcakes and pan-fried golden. Served with a lemon mayo and a pile of buttered peas. British seaside-meets-weeknight.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 300 g Salmon fillet (skinless)
  • 1 medium Lemon
  • 4 tbsp Mayonnaise
  • 150 g Frozen peas
  • 30 g Plain flour
  • 400 g Maris Piper potatoes
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 3 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • 2 tbsp Fresh dill
  • 60 g Panko breadcrumbs
  • 30 g Butter
  • 1 tsp Dijon mustard
  • 3 stalks Spring onion
  • to taste Salt

Instructions

  1. Peel and chunk the potatoes. Boil in salted water 15-18 minutes until knife-tender. Drain very well, let steam off in the colander 5 minutes.
  2. Tip the potatoes back into the pan. Add 15g butter, salt and pepper. Crush with a fork (not a masher — you want texture, not silk).
  3. If using fresh salmon, season and pan-fry 3-4 minutes per side or microwave 4 minutes. Flake into chunks. (Or use drained tinned salmon — easier.)
  4. Mix the crushed potato with the flaked salmon, chopped spring onion, chopped dill, lemon zest, mustard, salt and pepper. Don't overmix — keep the salmon in visible chunks.
  5. Divide into 4 portions. Shape each into a thick patty (about 2cm thick).
  6. Set up 2 plates: seasoned flour and panko breadcrumbs. Dredge each fishcake in flour first, then press firmly into the panko on top, bottom and sides. Chill 15 minutes (firmer, easier to fry).
  7. Heat 3 tbsp sunflower oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat. Fry the fishcakes 3-4 minutes per side until deep golden and crisp. Lift onto kitchen paper.
  8. Mix the lemon mayo: mayonnaise, lemon juice, chopped dill, salt and pepper. Plate the fishcakes with mayo dolloped alongside, buttered peas, lemon wedge.
