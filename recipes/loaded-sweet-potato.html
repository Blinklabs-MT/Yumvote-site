Loaded Sweet Potato

Roasted sweet potato split open and loaded with pulled BBQ chicken, melted cheese, black beans, sour cream and pickled jalapeños. Southern-meets-Tex-Mex meal-in-a-skin.

75 minEasy~549 kcal / servingAmerican
Contains dairy

Ingredients · per serving

  • 1 medium Lime
  • 4 medium Sweet potato
  • 1 tbsp Olive oil
  • 80 ml Sour cream
  • 4 tbsp Pickled jalapeños
  • to taste Hot sauce
  • 100 g Mature cheddar
  • 250 g Cooked chicken breast
  • ½ tsp Ground cumin
  • 20 g Butter
  • 3 tbsp Fresh coriander
  • 2 stalks Spring onion
  • to taste Salt
  • 80 ml BBQ sauce
  • ½ tsp Smoked paprika
  • 240 g Tinned black beans
