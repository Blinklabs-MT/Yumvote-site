Smothered Pork Chops

Pork chops pan-seared then braised in a rich onion-mushroom gravy until fork-tender, served over creamy mashed potato. Soul food classic with deep weeknight comfort.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 400 ml Chicken stock
  • 3 tbsp Plain flour
  • 2 tbsp Fresh parsley
  • 600 g Maris Piper potatoes
  • 200 g Mushrooms
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 1 tbsp Sunflower oil
  • 200 g Green beans
  • 60 g Butter
  • 1 tsp Worcestershire sauce
  • 1 tsp Dried thyme
  • 4 chops Pork chop (bone-in)
  • to taste Salt
  • 100 ml Double cream
  • 1 tsp Smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp Garlic powder
  • 80 ml Whole milk
  • 2 medium Onion

Instructions

  1. Pat the pork chops dry. Season generously with salt, pepper, smoked paprika and garlic powder. Dust lightly with flour, pat off excess.
  2. Heat 1 tbsp sunflower oil in a wide heavy pan over medium-high heat. Sear the chops 3-4 min per side until deep gold. Lift onto a plate.
  3. Lower heat to medium. Melt the butter in the pan. Add thinly sliced onions and a pinch of salt. Cook 12-15 minutes, stirring occasionally, until deep gold and jammy.
  4. Add the sliced mushrooms, cook 5 min until softened.
  5. Add minced garlic, dried thyme and remaining flour. Stir 60 seconds.
  6. Slowly whisk in the chicken stock and Worcestershire sauce. Bring to a simmer, will thicken 3-4 min.
  7. Stir in the double cream. Return the chops with any plate juices. Cover, simmer over low heat 20-25 minutes until the chops are fork-tender.
  8. Boil the potatoes 15 min until knife-tender. Drain. Mash with butter, milk, salt and pepper. Plate the mash, lay a chop on top, spoon the onion-mushroom gravy generously over. Steamed green beans alongside, scatter parsley.
