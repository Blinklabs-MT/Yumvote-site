Avocado Toast with Soft Egg

Smashed avocado on sourdough with a soft-boiled egg, chili flakes, lemon zest and flaky salt.

12 minEasy~376 kcal / servingBright Plates

Ingredients · per serving

  • ¼ lemon Lemon
  • 1 avocado Avocado
  • to taste Black pepper
  • pinch Chilli flakes
  • small handful Microgreens
  • 1 tsp Olive oil
  • 1 egg Egg
  • 1 slice Sourdough bread
  • to taste Flaky sea salt
  • pinch Sesame seeds

Instructions

  1. Bring water to boil. Lower egg in gently. Cook 6.5 min for runny. Plunge in cold water, peel.
  2. Toast sourdough deep gold + crispy.
  3. Mash avocado with lemon juice + salt + pepper. Keep some texture.
  4. Pile on toast. Halve egg, lay on top so yolk shows.
  5. Drizzle olive oil. Sprinkle chili flakes + lemon zest + flaky salt + sesame + microgreens.
  6. Eat immediately — yolk breaks at first cut.
