Pierogi with Caramelised Onions & Sour Cream

Polish potato-and-cheese dumplings boiled then pan-fried in butter, scattered with deeply caramelised onions and crowned with cool sour cream. Eastern European soul food.

Ingredients · per serving

  • 8 pieces Pierogi
  • 1 tsp Sunflower oil
  • 40 ml Sour cream
  • 40 g Smoked bacon
  • 30 g Butter
  • to taste Fresh chives
  • to taste Salt
  • 2 medium Onion

Instructions

  1. Peel the onions and slice into half-moons. Heat 1 tbsp butter and 1 tsp oil in a wide pan over medium-low heat. Add the onions with a pinch of salt.
  2. Cook slowly, stirring every few minutes, for 20-25 minutes until deep amber and jammy. If they start to colour too fast, lower the heat — patience here is everything.
  3. While the onions cook, bring a large pot of salted water to a gentle boil (not rolling — it tears the dumplings).
  4. Drop the pierogi in and stir gently to stop sticking. They're done when they float plus 1 more minute — usually 4-5 minutes for shop-bought.
  5. Drain the pierogi well. Heat the remaining butter in a wide pan over medium-high heat. Tip the pierogi in and fry 2 minutes per side until golden and slightly blistered.
  6. Pile the pierogi on a plate. Spoon the caramelised onions on top, dollop sour cream alongside, scatter with chopped chives and crispy bacon if using.
  7. Hit with a pinch of flaky salt and serve straight away — they cool fast and the crust softens.
