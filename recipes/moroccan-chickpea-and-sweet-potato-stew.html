Moroccan Chickpea & Sweet Potato Stew

Spiced chickpea and sweet potato stew with apricots and tomato. Sweet, warming, and entirely from the pantry.

40 minMedium~300 kcal / servingMediterranean
Contains nutsveganvegetariangluten freedairy free

Ingredients · per serving

  • 300 ml Vegetable stock
  • ¼ lemon Lemon
  • 1 tbsp Flaked almonds
  • 200 g Sweet potato
  • to taste Black pepper
  • 3 cloves Garlic
  • 40 g Dried apricots
  • 1 tsp Ground coriander
  • 1.5 tbsp Olive oil
  • 1 tsp grated Fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp Ground cumin
  • small handful Fresh coriander
  • 240 g Tinned chickpeas
  • to taste Salt
  • ½ tsp Ground cinnamon
  • 200 g Tinned chopped tomatoes
  • ½ tsp Smoked paprika
  • ¼ tsp Ground turmeric
  • 1 onion Onion
